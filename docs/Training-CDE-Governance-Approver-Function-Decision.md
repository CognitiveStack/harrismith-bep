### 2.1 Where the authority comes from

> **The authority arises from AG-002 itself.**

**This decision does not rely on any pre-existing independent authority of the
Training Implementation Owner.** The Increment 8G-R review established that the
Training Implementation Owner has **no independently established authority** — it
appears in the repository only as the **functional holder** of functions
established by other decisions, and no record defines its own scope, powers or
limits.

**AG-002 therefore creates the authority it confers.** It does not discover,
assume, infer or extend one.

### 2.2 Nature of the function

**This is a simulated training / reference-implementation function under TA-02.
It is not a real project appointment**, and it creates **no professional
authority, contractual duty or liability** for any person exercising it.

**No personal holder is recorded**, consistent with AG-001 §2 and the standing
repository convention.

## 3. Authority scope

The Training CDE Governance Approver **may**:

1. **receive and review** a defined CDE-structure governance proposal for the
   training implementation;
2. **decide whether the as-found CDE structure recorded by OF-001 is adopted,
   amended or replaced**;
3. **approve, reject, defer, or approve with explicitly recorded conditions** a
   defined intended CDE-structure proposal;
4. **establish the intended governance position** for:
   - **Common Files**;
   - **WIP**;
   - **Shared**;
   - **Published / Authorised**;
   - **Record / Retained** — while preserving that this **need not be a literal
     root folder** (BEP 6.3; CDE Workflow & State Strategy §1);
5. **determine the intended relationship** between conceptual information states
   and platform areas or containers;
6. **record the exact scope and basis** of the decision;
7. **authorise the controlled governance-status transition** from **observed
   as-found structure** to **approved intended governance**.

### 3.1 How the function is exercised

> **The function may decide OF-001 only through a later controlled decision
> record.**

**No OF-001 decision is made in Increment 8G-A.** The mechanism established here
is available; it has not been used.

## 7. Decision versus implementation

```text
AG-002
establishes who may decide the intended CDE structure.

A later OF-001 decision
establishes the approved intended CDE structure.

A later implementation authorisation
permits technical changes to the Autodesk environment.

Later verification
confirms that authorised implementation matches the approved decision.
```

> **None of the latter three events occurs in Increment 8G-A.**

**Deciding is not implementing.** BEP 5.9 — *"CDE Administration implements
governance; it does not create it. Changing the software does not make a
decision."* The converse holds equally: **making a decision changes no
software**, and AG-002 authorises no technical change of any kind.

## 8. Publication hold

> **PUBLICATION REMAINS NOT AUTHORISED — PUBLICATION HOLD ACTIVE.**

AG-002 does **not** lift or vary the publication hold, does **not** authorise
publication, upload, issue, delivery, receipt or acceptance, and does **not**
resolve **GCR-005**.

**A CDE-structure decision is not a publication decision.** Deciding where
information may be governed is a different act from authorising that any
particular information be published there.
