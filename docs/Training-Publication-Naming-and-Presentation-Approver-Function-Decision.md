established.

Implementation Owner as its functional holder** for the simulated training
implementation.

### 2.1 Where the authority comes from

> **The authority arises from AG-005 itself.**

**No pre-existing independent authority of the Training Implementation Owner is
assumed.** The Training Implementation Owner appears in this repository only as
the **functional holder** of functions established by other decisions, and no
authority is inferred from:

- the Training Implementation Owner label;
- repository ownership;
- project membership;
- system access;
- administrative permissions;
- **AG-001**, **AG-002**, **AG-003**, **AG-004**, **CGD-001** or **PAD-001**.

**AG-005 therefore creates the authority it confers**, as AG-002 §2.1,
AG-003 §2.1 and AG-004 §2.1 did before it.

### 2.2 Nature of the function

**This is a simulated training / reference-implementation function under TA-02.
It is not a real project appointment**, and it creates **no professional
authority, contractual duty or liability** for any person exercising it.

---

## 3. Purpose of the function

**The Training Publication Naming and Presentation Approver is the function that
may later decide the minimum package-specific naming and presentation controls
required by PAD-001 condition C5.**

The function exists to create a **controlled decision route for C5** without:

- **treating AG-004 coordination as decision authority**;
- **establishing project-wide standards**;
- **implementing filenames, files, folders or packages**;
- **authorising publication**.

> **Deciding what a thing must be called is not creating it, and neither is
> publishing it.**

---

## 4. Authority scope

**Through a later controlled decision**, the Training Publication Naming and
Presentation Approver **may**:

1. **receive a defined package-specific naming and presentation control
   candidate**;
2. **review its traceability** to PAD-001, C5 and the approved package boundary;
3. **approve** the candidate;
4. **reject** it;
5. **defer** it;
6. **approve it with explicitly recorded conditions**;
7. **define minimum naming controls for the seven `PDF/A-2b` renditions**;
8. **define minimum naming controls for the authoritative `UTF-8 JSON`
   manifest**;
9. **define minimum naming controls for the seven subordinate `UTF-8 Markdown`
   source companions**;
10. **define the required filename relationship** between each source companion
    and its corresponding authoritative rendition;
11. **define the minimum controlled name or naming rule for the proposed PM-1
    child container**;
12. **define permitted separators, character rules, case rules, extension
    treatment and token ordering**;
13. **define minimum package-specific identity, version, status and relationship
    tokens**;
14. **define minimum presentation requirements** needed to identify each
    rendition and its governance status;
15. **confirm that the package-specific controls are internally coherent and do
    not alter PAD-001's approved package boundary**;
16. **determine through a later controlled decision whether C5 is satisfied**;
17. **determine whether P6 step 3 is satisfied** as a consequence of the
    approved C5 controls;
18. **record any residual condition** that must remain before package assembly
    or commit pinning.

### 4.1 Package-specific, not project-wide

**Every item above is bounded to the Training Baseline 0.1 package and the
proposed PM-1 child container.** **None of them establishes a project-wide
naming, coordinate, titleblock or template standard** (§5), and none of them
may be cited as one. **The four project standards remain Not established.**

### 4.2 The approved package boundary is an input, not a subject

**The boundary is PAD-001's.** AG-005 names what PAD-001 approved; it may not
change what was approved:

| Class | Count | Contents |
|---|---|---|
| **Authoritative files** | **8** | 7 × `PDF/A-2b` renditions + 1 × `UTF-8 JSON` manifest |
| **Subordinate source companions** | **7** | 7 × `UTF-8 Markdown` authoring sources |
| **Total package files** | **15** | |

**Item 15 is a coherence check against this boundary, not a power to vary it.**
**The Markdown companions remain non-authoritative and subordinate**, and
naming them does not promote them (§5).

## 6. Candidate precondition

> **Before AG-005 may exercise its approval authority, a controlled C5 candidate
> must exist.**

The later candidate must cover, **at minimum**:

1. the **seven `PDF/A-2b` renditions**;
2. the **one authoritative `UTF-8 JSON` manifest**;
3. the **seven subordinate Markdown companions**;
4. the **companion-to-rendition relationships**;
5. the **PM-1 proposed child container**;
6. **package identity**;
7. **baseline / version / status representation**;
8. **filename characters, separators, case, extensions and ordering**;
9. **minimum presentation markings**;
10. **collision and ambiguity risks**;
11. **the distinction between package-specific controls and project-wide
    standards**.

> **No such candidate is created in Increment 8H-B.**

**Approving controls without a candidate covering the above is outside the
function's scope**, in the same way AG-003 §4 places approval without its
readiness precondition outside the Training Publication Arrangement Approver's
scope.

controls; **file, folder and container creation requires CDE administration or
implementation authority, which remains unestablished.**

## 8. Function separation

Nine functions and authorities, deliberately kept apart.

### 8.1 Training Publication Naming and Presentation Approver — AG-005

- **may later decide package-specific C5 controls**;
- **does not implement them**;
- **does not publish anything**.

### 8.2 Training Baseline Publication Owner — AG-004

- **coordinates the controls** (AG-004 §4 item 7);
- **maintains package identity and readiness**;
- **may submit or coordinate a C5 candidate**;
- **does not approve the controls**.

### 8.3 Training Publication Arrangement Approver — AG-003

- **approved the arrangement at PE-2**;
- **is not exercised in Increment 8H-B**.

### 8.4 Publication / Exchange Authoriser

- would **authorise PE-3**;
- **remains UNRESOLVED**;
- **is not established by AG-005**.

### 8.5 Technical Uploader / Executor

- would **execute PE-4**;
- **remains unassigned**;
- **is not established by AG-005**.

### 8.6 CDE Administration / Implementation Authority

- would **authorise file, folder, container or permission changes**;
- **remains unestablished**;
- **is not established by AG-005**.

### 8.7 Recipient Acceptance Authority

- would act at **PE-8**;
- **remains UNRESOLVED**;
- **is not established by AG-005**.

### 8.8 Training Baseline Approver — AG-001

- **is not exercised in Increment 8H-B**.

### 8.9 Training CDE Governance Approver — AG-002

- **is not exercised in Increment 8H-B**.

> **A shared functional holder does not merge these authorities, and every later
> act must identify the exact function exercised.**

That rule is BEP 5.11 and 9.12, and it is carried by AG-001 §9, AG-002 §5.1,
AG-003 §5, AG-004 §6 and PAD-001 §1.1.
