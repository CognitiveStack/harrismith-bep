### 2.1 Where the authority comes from

> **The authority arises from AG-004 itself.**

**No pre-existing independent authority of the Training Implementation Owner is
assumed.** The Training Implementation Owner appears in this repository only as
the **functional holder** of functions established by other decisions, and no

- the Training Implementation Owner label;

**This is a simulated training / reference-implementation function under TA-02.
It is not a real project appointment**, and it creates **no professional
authority, contractual duty or liability** for any person exercising it.

## 3. Purpose of the function

**The Training Baseline Publication Owner owns the controlled identity,
preparation governance and readiness coordination of a future Training
Baseline 0.1 publication package.**

The function exists so that the arrangement approved by PAD-001 is translated
into a controlled package-preparation process **without collapsing preparation
into publication authorisation or execution**.

> **Owning a package is not authorising its publication, and neither is
> executing one.**

---

## 4. Authority scope

The Training Baseline Publication Owner **may**:

1. **maintain the authoritative interpretation of the package boundary approved
   by PAD-001**;
2. **identify the exact controlled source content** intended for the package;
3. **maintain the package file inventory**:
   - **seven authoritative `PDF/A-2b` renditions**;
   - **one authoritative `UTF-8 JSON` manifest**;
   - **seven subordinate `UTF-8 Markdown` source companions**;
4. **maintain the relationship between each source companion and its
   authoritative rendition**;
5. **coordinate satisfaction of PAD-001 conditions and prerequisites relevant to
   package preparation**;
6. **receive and review evidence** that required controls have been satisfied;
7. **coordinate minimum naming and presentation controls** established through a
   **separately authorised decision**;
8. **coordinate the selection and testing of generation and validation routes**
   established through **separately authorised technical controls**;
9. **identify when the repository state is eligible** for later
   publication-package commit pinning;
10. **propose the exact publication-package commit** for a later controlled
    pinning action;
11. **maintain the package identity and version record** after a commit is later
    pinned;
12. **coordinate manifest data preparation** using PAD-001's approved metadata
    requirements;
13. **prepare or coordinate a package-readiness record** for consideration by the
    future publication / exchange authoriser;
14. **identify unresolved conditions, prerequisites or stop conditions**;
15. **stop package preparation** where the approved controls are not satisfied;
16. **request a later PE-3 decision** from the appropriately established
    publication / exchange authority.

### 4.1 The approved package boundary

**The boundary is PAD-001's, not this function's.** It is recorded here only so
that the inventory the function maintains is unambiguous:

| Class | Count | Contents |
|---|---|---|
| **Authoritative files** | **8** | 7 × `PDF/A-2b` renditions + 1 × `UTF-8 JSON` manifest |
| **Subordinate source companions** | **7** | 7 × `UTF-8 Markdown` authoring sources |
| **Total package files** | **15** | |

**The Markdown companions remain non-authoritative and subordinate.** **The
Training Baseline Publication Owner may not alter this boundary** (§5).

### 4.2 How the function is exercised

> **Each act of the function must be recorded, must identify AG-004 as its
> authority basis, and must remain inside §4.**

**Requesting, proposing, coordinating and identifying are not deciding.**
Items 9, 10, 13 and 16 above are **preparatory acts addressed to another
authority** — they neither pin a commit, nor authorise an event, nor bind the
function that later receives them.

## 6. Function separation

Eight functions and authorities, deliberately kept apart.

### 6.1 Training Baseline Publication Owner — AG-004

- **owns package identity, preparation governance and readiness coordination**;
- **does not authorise publication**;
- **does not execute publication**;
- **does not accept publication**.

### 6.2 Training Publication Arrangement Approver — AG-003

- **approved the arrangement through PAD-001 at PE-2**;
- **is not exercised in Increment 8H-A**.

### 6.3 Publication / Exchange Authoriser

- would **authorise a specific publication event at PE-3**;
- **remains UNRESOLVED**;
- **is not established by AG-004**.

### 6.4 Technical Uploader / Executor

- would **execute PE-4** under separate authority;
- **remains unassigned**;
- **is not established by AG-004**.

### 6.5 CDE Administration / Implementation Authority

- would **authorise container creation, permissions or other platform changes**;
- **remains unestablished**;
- **is not established by AG-004**.

### 6.6 Recipient Acceptance Authority

- would act at **PE-8**;
- **remains UNRESOLVED**;
- **is not established by AG-004**, and **cannot be established by the
  publishing side**.

### 6.7 Training Baseline Approver — AG-001

- **approved Training Baseline candidate snapshots**;
- **is not exercised in Increment 8H-A**.

### 6.8 Training CDE Governance Approver — AG-002

- **decided the intended CDE root topology**;
- **is not exercised in Increment 8H-A**.

> **The same functional holder may hold several simulated training functions,
> but every act must identify the exact function and authority being
> exercised.**

That rule is BEP 5.11 and 9.12, and it is carried by AG-001 §9, AG-002 §5.1,
AG-003 §5 and PAD-001 §1.1.
