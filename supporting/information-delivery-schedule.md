# Information Delivery Schedule

## Document purpose and status

**Purpose.** Defines what information is delivered on the Harrismith Fire
Station project, by whom, to whom, in what form, and when — aligned to the
project's stages and decision points.

**Status.** *Scaffold only.* No deliverables and no dates have been recorded.

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Document status | Scaffold — not a baseline |
| Classification | PROPOSED GOVERNANCE (structure only) |
| Authority | None |
| Supports | BEP section 10 — Information Delivery and Exchange |

**Population rule.** This schedule is to be populated only from the confirmed
project programme and confirmed information requirements. Dates, stages,
milestones and deliverables are not to be invented, estimated, or inferred
from a typical programme. Where a date is not confirmed, it is recorded as an
UNRESOLVED DECISION rather than filled with a plausible value.
