# Coordination & Review Strategy

## Document purpose and status

**Purpose.** Defines how models and information are coordinated, checked,
reviewed, approved and authorised on the Harrismith Fire Station project —
including coordination cycles, clash and interface management, and the review
and approval route.

**Status.** *Scaffold only.* No coordination or review process has been
defined.

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Document status | Scaffold — not a baseline |
| Classification | PROPOSED GOVERNANCE (structure only) |
| Authority | None |
| Supports | BEP sections 8 and 9 — Model and Information Coordination; Review, Approval and Authorisation |

**Population rule.** Coordination cycles, review gates and acceptance criteria
are to be developed from confirmed project requirements and the observed
project setup. They are not to be inferred from generic practice and presented
as project governance.
