# Governance & Decision Register

## Document purpose and status

**Purpose.** The controlled record of governance decisions on the Harrismith
Fire Station project: what was decided, what remains open, what was assumed,
and what was observed but not yet resolved.

**Status.** *Scaffold only.* No governance decisions have been made or
approved.

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Document status | Scaffold — not a baseline |
| Classification | PROPOSED GOVERNANCE (structure only) |
| Authority | None |
| Supports | BEP section 12 — Governance, Change and Exceptions |

**Population rule.** Entries are added only from actual decisions, actual
observations, or explicitly declared assumptions. No entry is created to make
the register look complete. Every entry carries its classification: OBSERVED
FACT, TRAINING ASSUMPTION, PROPOSED GOVERNANCE, APPROVED GOVERNANCE or
UNRESOLVED DECISION.

**Standing rule.** Known live-project discrepancies are recorded here and are
**not silently corrected** elsewhere in the documentation set. A discrepancy
is resolved only by an explicit, recorded decision.
