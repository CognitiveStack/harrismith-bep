## 2. CDE concept review

- [ ] **Process versus platform** — the CDE is the governed process; the platform implements part of it.
- [ ] **Folder versus state** — the existence or name of an area establishes no information state.
- [ ] **Property distinctions** — state, version, revision, status and suitability answer five different questions, and none creates another.
- [ ] **State purposes** — four states, four distinct permitted purposes.
- [ ] **Permitted reliance** — who may rely on each state, and on what basis.
- [ ] **Transition authority** — who initiates, who checks, who authorises, and that they are three functions.
- [ ] **Transition evidence** — evidence is required, and it is not a folder location.
- [ ] **Publication block** — `T4` is blocked because the authority is unassigned, and information remains Shared.
- [ ] **Retention boundary** — the obligation is governed; the method is not; it is not a folder.
- [ ] **Configuration direction** — governance decides, configuration implements, and the reverse does not hold.

## 5. Teaching review

**Confirm the audience should leave understanding:**

- [ ] **the CDE is a governed process**, not a folder tree or a platform;
- [ ] **access does not create reliance** — visibility is not permission;
- [ ] **Shared is purpose-bound**, not general approval;
- [ ] **publication requires a separate authority**, and on this project nobody holds it;
- [ ] **retention is an obligation without an established CDE method**;
- [ ] **technical movement is not a transition**;
- [ ] **only `T1` and `T4` change information state**;
- [ ] **evidence matters** — a transition without it is not governed;
- [ ] **publication is not delivery**, and delivery is not receipt or acceptance;
- [ ] **governance precedes configuration**;
- [ ] **Triviron must answer project-specific questions** before configuring anything.
