## 1. Objective

**To be able to follow a coordination matter from a controlled input to a
verified disposition, say which act is being performed at each point, and state
precisely what has and has not been demonstrated — without completing the route
on the project's behalf.**

| **Module 4** | How does information move through controlled **states** and **transitions**? |

| **Module 6** | **What happened to it — who decided, what was verified, and what does the evidence actually show?** |

## 2. Central question

> **How does Harrismith govern the route from a coordination finding to a
> verified disposition — and which acts must stay distinct?**

**Classification: `INTERP`.** Assembled from `S1` §8, `S1` §9.2 and `S2`
§12–§21.

### 2.1 The provisional question was corrected

The increment proposed: *"How does Harrismith turn coordination findings into
controlled decisions, verified resolution and traceable closure — and which acts
remain distinct?"*

**"Turn … into" and "traceable closure" are not supported.** `S8` §8 records the
cycle as **`PARTIALLY TRACEABLE / NOT YET DEMONSTRATED AS A COMPLETE CYCLE`**,
and `GCR-006` remains **OPEN**. Harrismith **governs** the route; it has not been
shown to **perform** it. The wording now says *governs*, and ends at *verified
disposition* — `S2` §19's own term — rather than at *traceable closure*.

**The provisional wording was not preserved merely because it appeared in the
increment brief.**

## 3. The core conceptual distinction

**A record of a decision is not evidence that the decision was made, and a
status is not a technical fact.**

`S2` §19 states the working form of it:

> *"A material Issue is not closed solely because someone says it was fixed in
> WIP… a change nobody can see in Shared information has not been demonstrated."*

`S7` `OF-008` states the platform form of it:

> *"Environment configured ≠ coordination process executed."*
