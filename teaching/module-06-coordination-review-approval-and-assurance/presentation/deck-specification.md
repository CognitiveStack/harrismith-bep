## 7. Deck-wide accessibility rules

Every slide must carry: **logical reading order** · a **screen-reader
description** · **status meaning in words** · **no information by colour alone** ·
the type minimums in §4 · **connector meaning carried by direction and label** ·
**warnings legible at projection scale**.

**Anything a sighted reader takes from separation, enclosure, weight or position
must also be said in words** — the Slide 3 gap, the Slide 4 upward direction and
missing author label, the Slide 5 exit equality, the Slide 9 absent return
connector, the Slide 10 detachment, the Slide 12 empty boxes and the Slide 14
block equality. **None of those survive audio.**
