§13.6**, not by this module.

| # | Variance | `S1` | `S2` | Module 6 position |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | **Cycle-step wording** | §8.8 — *"input / readiness check → federation → coordination checks → triage findings → …"* | §17 — *"readiness check → federation → coordination checks → findings → triage → …"* and adds **"controlled Shared inputs"** at the head and **"retain evidence"** at the tail | **Both record the same governed route.** `S2` separates *findings* from *triage* as two steps where `S1` writes *"triage findings"*. **Module 6 teaches `S2`'s sequence because `S1` §8.13 defers the detail to it, and records that `S1` words it as one step** |
