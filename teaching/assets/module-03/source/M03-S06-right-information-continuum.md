## 1. Format decision — a layout, deliberately not Mermaid

**A continuum is a scale, not a graph.** Mermaid would render two failure
directions as a flow, and a flow has a destination — which is exactly the
minimalism reading the slide exists to prevent.

## 2. Layout specification — the continuum

```text
   TOO LITTLE                                              TOO MUCH
        │                                                       │
        ▼                                                       ▼
   ◄────┼───────────────[  PROPORTIONATE  ]────────────────────┼────►
        │                purpose-dependent                     │
        │                 ── movable ──                        │
        │                                                       │
   decision risk                                        cost, ambiguity,
   • decided in the dark,                               unmaintainable detail
     looking like daylight                              • coordinated, checked
   • clean result from an                                 and revised by someone
     incomplete model                                   • unwarranted confidence
   • re-surveyed later, at cost                         • three containers that
                                                          later disagree

   BOTH ENDS ARE FAILURES — drawn with equal weight
```

**Five requirements this layout carries.**

1. **The proportionate zone is a movable band, not a fixed point**, and is
   labelled **`Purpose-dependent`**. A fixed midpoint asserts a universal
   quantity, and no such quantity exists.
2. **Both ends carry equal visual weight.** *Too little → decision risk* is the
   half an over-corrected audience forgets.
3. **No green zone.** The proportionate band is neutral — colouring it green
   turns a proportionality argument into a conformity result (prohibition 18).
4. **No tick, no score, no percentage, no numbered level.**
5. **No level-of-information-need breakdown of any kind** — no geometric /
   alphanumeric / documentation split, no tiers, no grades. The plain concept may
   be **stated**; it may not be **structured** (prohibition 17).

## 3. Companion panel — proportionate to what?

**A table.** Six factors, no relationships between them to draw.

| # | Proportionate to | |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The decision** | What is actually being decided |
| 2 | **The recipient** | Who has to act on it |
| 3 | **The delivery event** | The exchange it serves |
| 4 | **The asset or element** | A door handle and a structural frame do not warrant the same treatment |
| 5 | **The required reliability** | How much weight the decision puts on it |
| 6 | **The stage or context of use** | What is knowable, and what is needed, at that point |

*(**Supported interpretation**, built from `H1` §7.3's requirement fields,
§10.1's delivery grid and §6.1's *sharing is purposeful* / *publication is
purposeful*. **No source lists these six** — `M3-S6-03`.)*

## 4. Optional supporting build — more versus right

**A supporting build on the proportionate zone only. Never the primary form.**

| **More information** | **Right information** |
|---|---|
| Produced because the software can | Supports an **identified decision** |
| Duplicated across containers | Has a **known originator** |
| Delivered without a defined purpose | Has a **defined recipient and purpose** |
| Detail that cannot be checked or maintained | Delivered **at the appropriate time** |
| Unclear recipient or use | Can be **checked, maintained and relied upon** |

***Teaching examples. Not ISO quotations, and not a conformity checklist.***
**If used, the two columns are the same height** — a fat left column beside a lean
right one is a picture of minimalism, whatever the words say.

## 5. The Harrismith marker

Placed on the proportionate zone, as a labelled gap:

> **Level of information need: not defined on this project.** Not established for
> any container, and **not inferred from observed model content**. Recorded as an
> information gap. *(`H2` matrix §4)*

**The second clause is the sharper teaching.** What is in the models is not what
was required — the same move as *folder names do not prove governance*, applied to
model detail.

**Hard limit: no model-detail requirement for the fire station is drawn, implied
or exemplified.** None exists, and a hypothetical becomes a production expectation
two meetings later.

## 6. Simplify and omit

| Simplify | Omit |
|---|---|
| One continuum, one movable band, two labelled ends | **Any green zone, tick, score, percentage or numbered level** |
| Six factors as a companion table | Any file-count or storage-volume figure — none exists |
| One Harrismith marker | **Any level, grade, scale or tier** |
| The two-column build only if the zone needs unpacking | **Any fire-station model-detail example** |

## 7. Overclaim risk

**MEDIUM-HIGH, in two directions.** Weighted toward the left, it teaches
minimalism; styled with a good end and a bad end, it becomes a scorecard. The
continuum with two failure ends and a movable neutral band is the form that
avoids both.
