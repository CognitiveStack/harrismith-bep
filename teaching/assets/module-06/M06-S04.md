## 1. Purpose and five-second takeaway

**Purpose.** Fix the boundary around federation before any finding is discussed:
aggregation transfers no authorship, technical ownership or deliverable status.

**Five-second takeaway.** *The arrows point up. Nothing flows down into the
federation.*

**Why this form.** **Direction is the whole argument.** An upward connector says
*this view is composed of those*; a downward connector says *ownership arrives
here*. The originator labels on the containers and their **absence** on the view
make the asymmetry visible rather than asserted.

**Simplification rule.** Container labels may reduce to their refs — `ARC-01`
`STR-01` `MEC-01` `ELE-01` `PLM-01` `FIR-01`. **No connector may lose its
`assembled from` label**, **no originator label may be dropped**, and
**`COORD-01` may not be moved above the containers or given an author.**

## 4. Fixed layout

**Six source containers along the upper portion**, each carrying a **visible
originator label**.

**One federated coordination view beneath them**, carrying **no author label**.

**Connectors run from the federated view upward to the source containers**, every

## 7. Connector semantics

**Every connector means `assembled from` and points upward, from the view to a
container.**

**No connector points downward, and no connector returns.**

The `COORD-01` line is **dashed, unarrowed**, and means *"this is the named
coordination construct for that view"* — **never ownership, never authorship,
never containment.**

**Six upward `assembled from` connectors. One dashed unarrowed `COORD-01` line.
No other connector.**

## 8. Status and warning treatment

**Mandatory warning: `Federation transfers no authorship or technical
ownership`**, full width and **visually equal to the diagram**, plus the
implementation line recording **zero coordinated versions** and **no demonstrated
run**.

**The federated view carries no author label**, and that absence is itself a
status statement — it must be described in words in the alt text.

**Status is carried by words, never by colour alone**, and never by a coloured
chip, badge or dot. Minimum visible type **14 pt**; principal labels **≥ 18 pt**;
text contrast **≥ 4.5 : 1**; border and connector contrast **≥ 3 : 1**.

## 13. Producer-failure test

**Ask a reader which direction responsibility travels.** If any answer is *into
the federation*, the connectors have failed.

**Ask who authored the federated view.** If a reader names anyone, the missing
author label has failed.

**Ask how many organisations the slide shows.** If the answer is *six*, the
wording has failed — **six discipline domains are not six organisations.**

## 14. STOP conditions

**Applicable module-wide STOP conditions: 1, 11, 12, 13, 14, 52, 53, 54, 59, 65,
66, 70, 72.**

Stop and refer back to the accepted plan if this visual would:

- Imply the complete governed coordination cycle has been executed **(1)**
- **Transfer authorship or technical ownership into a federation (11)**
- Make the BIM Coordinator the design author or the owner of the fix **(12)**
- Represent `COORD-01` as automatically jointly authored, or as a formal
  deliverable **(13)**
- **Call the federation a "single source of truth" (14)**
- Convert a role into a person or organisation **(52)**
- Rely on colour alone **(53)**
- Import external imagery or a screenshot **(54)**
- Present six discipline domains as six organisations, six platform teams or six
  appointments **(59)**
- Show the federated coordination view being edited to implement a fix **(65)**
- Show `COORD-01` replacing, superseding or standing in for the six discipline
  containers **(66)**
- Use a coloured chip, badge, dot or traffic light in place of a status word
  **(70)**
- Name a holder, organisation or appointment **(72)**

The full seventy-five module-wide STOP conditions are in [`README.md`](README.md)
§8 and in the accepted plan §3.
