## 1. Purpose and five-second takeaway

**Purpose.** Show the seven recorded triage outcomes and prevent the module's
most damaging word-collision.

**Five-second takeaway.** *Seven outcomes. One of them is a trap.*

**Why this form.** Equal rows prevent any disposition reading as the normal or
preferred outcome. **The warning glyph must sit *in the row*, so the caution
cannot be separated from the term it qualifies.**

**Simplification rule.** The *what it records* column may compress. **The seven
disposition names are recorded wording and may not be renamed, paraphrased,
merged or reordered.** **The caution may not be shortened**, and **the four-row
`is not` block may not lose a row.**

## 7. Connector semantics

**One leader line only**, from the caution call-out to the `Accepted condition`
row. It means **`this caution applies to this row`** and nothing else.

**No arrows between dispositions** — they are alternatives, not a sequence.

## 8. Status and warning treatment

**Mandatory warning: the `Accepted condition` caution, worded and positioned as a
caution, never as a recommendation or a tip.** It must not use language such as
*note*, *remember* or *helpfully*.

The call-out's border is **heavier than the table's**, so it reads as a **warning
frame rather than emphasis**.

**The warning glyph must have a text equivalent** — a glyph alone does not
survive audio.

**Status is carried by words, never by colour alone**, and never by a coloured
chip, badge or dot. Minimum visible type **14 pt**; principal labels **≥ 18 pt**;
text contrast **≥ 4.5 : 1**; border and connector contrast **≥ 3 : 1**.

## 9. Build or reveal sequence

1. Seven rows **at equal height**.
2. Warning glyph in the `Accepted condition` row.
3. Caution call-out with all six lines.
4. Leader line.
5. Four-row `is not` block.
6. Status.

## 10. Mandatory omissions

- **No eighth row**
- **No renamed or merged disposition**
- No arrow between dispositions
- No preferred styling
- No Issue status shown alongside a disposition
- No colour-only signalling
- No invented identifier

## 11. Accessibility and projection requirements

**Reading order:** row 1 → row 7, each read with its recorded meaning → **the
caution read immediately after `Accepted condition`, not at the end** → four-row
block → status.

**The warning glyph must have a text equivalent.**

No information is carried by colour alone. Warnings remain legible at projection
scale.

## 13. Producer-failure test

**Count the rows.** If the answer is not exactly seven, **STOP.**

**Ask a reader whether `Accepted condition` means the client accepted it.** If
the answer is anything but a clear *no*, the caution has failed.

**Ask whether the call-out reads as advice or as a warning.** If advice, redraw.
