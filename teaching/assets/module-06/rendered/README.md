## 2. Why nothing is rendered — and why there is nothing to render

**All fourteen Module 6 visuals are native-layout specifications.** Each states
geometry in points, exact labels, connector count and direction, and elements
that are deliberately **empty, detached, unequal or refused**.

**There is no diagram source to render.** `Mermaid PROHIBITED × 14` was decided
visual by visual in the accepted plan §2.5 and restated in each source file §15,
because automatic graph layout could falsely imply **hierarchy · ownership
transfer · a completed cycle · an automatic Issue · a mandatory status sequence ·
an approved tolerance · verification in every cycle · closure as proof · a
Triviron answer.**

**That is a design decision, not a gap.** A renderer that tidied any of those
would assert something the sources decline to assert.

## 3. What a future render or production process may not do

**Module 6 depends on things being visibly absent, separated, unequal or
incomplete.** A render, layout engine, template or production step **may not**:

| # | Prohibition |
|---:|---|
| 1 | **Close the Slide 3 route** into a loop, or join the route band to the implementation-evidence panel |
| 2 | **Reverse Slide 4 connector direction**, or add an author label to the federated view |
| 3 | **Rank Slide 5 outcomes** — the two exits stay geometrically identical |
| 4 | **Fill Slide 6 empty cells**, shade them to look decided, or remove them |
| 5 | **Automate Slide 7 into an Issue conveyor**, or make either outcome the default path |
| 6 | **Add an eighth Slide 8 row**, or merge, rename or reorder any of the seven |
| 7 | **Add a Slide 9 return connector** of any kind, in any style, with any label |
| 8 | **Place Slide 10 alternates in sequence**, or number `Deferred` and `Escalated` as seven and eight |
| 9 | **Make Slide 11 verification universal** — the gate keeps `where required`, and the `H18` warning stays visible |
| 10 | **Tick Slide 12 boxes**, shade them, or add a percentage, progress bar or green |
| 11 | **Mark Slide 13 outputs produced**, or reduce the assurance-absence strip below the weight of the three blocks |
| 12 | **Populate Slide 14 answer areas** with any value, Harrismith or otherwise, or draw any arrow into Triviron |
| 13 | **Replace status words with colour or icons** — every status is carried by a **word**, on every slide |

**A render never becomes authoritative.** Where a render and its source differ,
**the source wins**; where a source and the accepted plan differ, **the plan
wins**; where the plan and the accepted teaching content differ, **the teaching
content wins**; where any teaching material conflicts with a controlled
Harrismith source, **the controlled source wins**.

## 4. If rendering is ever undertaken

1. **Render from committed source, unchanged.** No silent correction, no
   tidying, no completion of a deliberately incomplete element.
2. **Name the output after its source identifier** — `M06-S07.svg` for
   `M06-S07.md`.
3. **Prefer SVG**, at the resolution the presentation actually needs.
4. **Update [`../visual-register.md`](../visual-register.md)** with the rendered
   status.
5. **Check every prohibition in §3 against the output** before committing it.
6. **Record the renderer and its version**, so the result is reproducible.

**None of this has been done, and none of it is authorised by this file.**
