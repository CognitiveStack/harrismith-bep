## 1. Purpose and five-second takeaway

**Purpose.** Separate **coordinating an assignment** from **owning a technical
solution**, and keep the fix in the originating task team's own WIP.

**Five-second takeaway.** *One arrow down. Nothing comes back up.*

**Why this form.** Lanes make the boundary structural. **A single downward arrow
is the entire claim of the slide:** coordination hands over **work**, not
**authorship**. **Any upward connector — even one labelled *reports* — would be
read as authorship or responsibility travelling back.**

**Simplification rule.** Lane activity labels may compress to single words. **The
lanes may never merge, overlap or be enclosed in a shared frame**; **the
`assigns` connector may never lose its label or gain a counterpart**; **`where
established` and `only where genuinely established` must be printed in full**,
never abbreviated to a symbol or a footnote marker.

## 4. Fixed layout

**Two horizontal lanes that never merge, never overlap and never share a border.**

| Lane | Label | Contents |
|---|---|---|
| Upper | **BIM Coordinator — coordination process** | assignment · facilitation · monitoring |
| Lower | **Originating task team** | WIP correction · task-team check · controlled reshare |

**Exactly one connector between the lanes**, labelled **`assigns`**, pointing
**from the coordination lane down to the task-team lane**.

**No connector returns upward.**

The **seven assignment fields** sit as a plain list beside or beneath the lanes.

## 7. Connector semantics

**Exactly one connector on the slide.**

It means **`assigns`** — a coordination act transferring **work**, not
authorship — and it points **downward only**.

**No return connector, no bidirectional arrow, no dashed feedback line, and no
connector labelled *reports*, *reshares* or *informs*.**

**Controlled reshare is shown as a step *inside* the lower lane**, never as an
arrow back to the upper lane.

## 8. Status and warning treatment

**Mandatory warning: `Coordinating an assignment and owning a solution are
different things`**, placed **on or immediately beside the `assigns`
connector**, and the **`NOT DEMONSTRATED`** status line.

**The two qualifier phrases — `where established` and `only where genuinely
established` — are printed in full**, never reduced to an asterisk or a footnote
marker. They are status wording, not decoration.

**Status is carried by words, never by colour alone**, and never by a coloured
chip, badge or dot. Minimum visible type **14 pt**; principal labels **≥ 18 pt**;
text contrast **≥ 4.5 : 1**; border and connector contrast **≥ 3 : 1**.

## 11. Accessibility and projection requirements

**Reading order:** upper lane → lower lane → **the connector, its direction and
its label, stated in words** → the *coordinating is not owning* warning → seven

## 13. Producer-failure test

**Count the connectors between the lanes.** If the answer is not exactly one,
**STOP.**

**Ask a reader who owns the fix.** If any answer is *the BIM Coordinator*, the
lanes have failed.

**Search the slide for a date, a priority value or an identifier.** If one
exists, **STOP.**

## 14. STOP conditions

**Applicable module-wide STOP conditions: 12, 26, 27, 52, 53, 54, 63, 64, 65, 70,
72.**

Stop and refer back to the accepted plan if this visual would:

- **Make the BIM Coordinator the design author or the owner of the fix (12)**
- **Invent an Issue identifier, due date, target, priority, person or
  organisation (26)**
- **Transfer technical authorship through assignment (27)**
- Convert a role into a person or organisation **(52)**
- Rely on colour alone **(53)**
- Import external imagery or a screenshot **(54)**
- Show verification, acceptance or closure transferring technical or design
  responsibility away from the originating task team **(63)**
- **Show a verbal agreement, meeting or discussion modifying controlled
  information (64)**
- **Show the federated coordination view being edited to implement a fix (65)**
- Use a coloured chip, badge, dot or traffic light in place of a status word
  **(70)**
- Name a holder, organisation or appointment **(72)**

The full seventy-five module-wide STOP conditions are in [`README.md`](README.md)
§8 and in the accepted plan §3.
