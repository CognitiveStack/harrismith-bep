## 1. Purpose and five-second takeaway

**Purpose.** Establish the source-supported cycle **and its recorded
implementation position in the same view**, so no implementation belief is
carried through the module.

**Five-second takeaway.** *One governed route. Not yet demonstrated.*

**Why this form.** Fragments are what was observed. **Joining them would draw a
cycle nobody has run.** The gap is the argument: it is the **visible distance**
between a governed route and what has been shown.

**Simplification rule.** Route step labels may compress to two or three words
each, and the fifteen steps may wrap to two lines. **No step may be deleted,
reordered or merged**; **`S1` §8.8's one-step wording of *findings* and *triage*
must be recorded as a note, not applied to the route**; **no evidence line may be
dropped, and none may be summarised into a count.**

## 6. Geometry or spatial relationships

**Reference canvas: `960 × 540 pt`, 16:9. Origin top left; `x` increases right,
`y` increases down. Title zone `y = 0–72` is reserved. Side margins ≥ 48 pt.**

Route band `x = 48–912`, `y = 96–236`, steps as equal-height boxes on one or two
rows with **short arrows between adjacent steps only**.

**Gap strip `x = 48–912`, `y = 252–300`**, full width, **visibly separating the
bands**.

Evidence panel `x = 48–912`, `y = 316–492`, **one outer frame containing four or
five separate record blocks with ≥ 16 pt clear space between them and no
connectors**.

**Route band and evidence panel never touch and are never enclosed together.**

## 7. Connector semantics

**Inside the route only:** a short arrow means **the governed route's next step**
and nothing else.

**The route's last step does not connect back to its first — no loop is drawn.**

**No connector of any kind enters or leaves the evidence panel**, and **no
connector crosses the gap strip.**

## 8. Status and warning treatment

**Mandatory warning: the gap strip's three status words together**, and the
**absence-of-observation sentence inside or immediately beneath the evidence
panel's frame**.

**The gap is load-bearing.** It is not whitespace to be tightened; it is the
visible distance between governance and evidence, and the strip inside it is a
principal object.

**Status is carried by words, never by colour alone**, and never by a coloured
chip, badge or dot. Minimum visible type **14 pt**; principal labels **≥ 18 pt**;
text contrast **≥ 4.5 : 1**; border and connector contrast **≥ 3 : 1**.

## 9. Build or reveal sequence

1. Route heading **with its governance label**.
2. Route steps.
3. Variance note.
4. **Gap strip with all three status words.**
5. Evidence panel **with its records already separated**.
6. Absence-of-observation line.

## 10. Mandatory omissions

- **No closed loop**
- No percentage, no progress bar, no green, no tick
- No "in progress" marker
- No platform screenshot
- **No line joining any two observations**
- No discipline shown as absent or behind

## 11. Accessibility and projection requirements

**Reading order:** route heading → route steps in order → variance note → gap
strip → evidence records one by one → absence-of-observation line.

**The gap must be described in words.** Arrow direction is stated in the alt
text, never inferred. **Each evidence record is read with its own status.**

No information is carried by colour alone. Warnings remain legible at projection
scale.

## 13. Producer-failure test

**Cover the route band.** Can a reader state that the cycle has **not** been
demonstrated, and that `GCR-006` is open? If not, the evidence panel has failed.

**Then cover the evidence panel.** Does the route alone look like a description
of current practice? If yes, the governance heading has failed.

**Finally: is there any line, arrow or shared border between the two bands?** If
yes, **STOP.**
