## 1. Purpose and five-second takeaway

**Purpose.** Show the governed status model, keep its two alternate dispositions
out of the main sequence, and stop a status being read as a state, a code, an
approval or a technical fact.

**Five-second takeaway.** *A status records where a matter sits. Nothing else.*

**Why this form.** **Detachment is the argument.** A matter does not pass
*through* `Deferred` or `Escalated` on its way to `Closed`, so they cannot sit on
the line. **Their leaders must attach to the sequence as a whole**, to avoid
implying a specific exit point the source does not state.

**Simplification rule.** The `is not` block's explanations may compress. **The
six statuses may not be renamed, reordered, reduced or extended**; **`Deferred`
and `Escalated` may never be numbered, in-lined, or given sequence connectors**;
**the three warnings may not be shortened.**

## 7. Connector semantics

**Sequence arrows mean `next governed status` and point one way only.**

**The leaders to the detached block are unlabelled, unarrowed, and mean only
`related, controlled, and not in the sequence`.**

**No arrow enters or leaves `Deferred` or `Escalated` as a sequence step**, and
**no arrow returns from `Closed`.**

## 8. Status and warning treatment

**Mandatory warning: the three warnings together, at ≥ 18 pt**, and the
**`Not a seventh and eighth stage`** label inside the detached block.

**No coloured status chips or badges anywhere** — a chip is exactly the rendering
`S2` §15 warns about, and it would substitute a colour for a governed meaning.

**Status is carried by words, never by colour alone.** Minimum visible type
**14 pt**; principal labels **≥ 18 pt**; text contrast **≥ 4.5 : 1**; border and
connector contrast **≥ 3 : 1**.

## 13. Producer-failure test

**Ask a reader how many stages the model has.** If the answer is *eight*, the
detachment has failed.

**Ask whether the model is configured in the platform.** If the answer is *yes*
or *probably*, the warning has failed.

**Ask what `Closed` proves.** If the answer includes *fixed* or *verified*, the
`Closed label ≠ proof` warning has failed.
