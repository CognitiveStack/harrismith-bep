## 1. Purpose and five-second takeaway

**Purpose.** Transfer the **method** and answer nothing on Triviron's behalf.

**Five-second takeaway.** *Every answer area reads: not yet established.*

**Why this form.** **A permanently empty, visibly bordered answer area in every
group is what makes the slide structurally incapable of asserting a Triviron
answer.** Identical treatment prevents any group reading as nearer to an answer
than another.

**Simplification rule.** Questions may reduce to keywords. **No answer area may
be removed, resized, restyled or filled — including with a Harrismith example.**
**No group may be added, merged, renamed or reordered.** **The transfer table's
two columns may never differ in width, weight or type size.**

## 4. Fixed layout

**Seven question groups**, arranged as a structured grid.

**Every group carries a clearly visible bordered answer area**, and **every answer
area reads `NOT YET ESTABLISHED` and nothing else**.

**All seven answer areas are identical in size, border, position within their
group and type treatment.**

Beneath: a **balanced transfer table — what transfers, what does not transfer —
two columns of equal weight**.

A **final boundary strip** across the foot.

## 6. Geometry or spatial relationships

**Reference canvas: `960 × 540 pt`, 16:9. Origin top left; `x` increases right,
`y` increases down. Title zone `y = 0–72` is reserved. Side margins ≥ 48 pt.**

Seven group blocks in a structured grid across `x = 48–912`, `y = 96–388` —
**four in an upper row, three in a lower row, all of identical height; the lower
row's fourth position is left empty rather than resized**, so no group is
enlarged by position.

**Answer area = the lower 34 pt of each block, bordered, containing only
`NOT YET ESTABLISHED` at ≥ 14 pt.**

Transfer table `x = 48–912`, `y = 400–474`, **two columns of exactly equal width
≈ 432 pt**.

Boundary strip `x = 48–912`, `y = 484–520`.

**Grid position must not imply priority**: no group is numbered first by size,
colour or emphasis, only by its recorded number.

## 7. Connector semantics

**None anywhere on the slide.**

No connector between groups, none between the transfer columns, and — above all —
**no arrow from any Harrismith item into any Triviron question**, which would be
**a recommendation drawn as a line**.

## 8. Status and warning treatment

**Mandatory warning: `NOT YET ESTABLISHED` × 7**, and the two-line final
boundary.

**Every answer area is permanently empty apart from its status text.** No
Harrismith value may enter one, as an example, a default or an illustration.

**Status is carried by words, never by colour alone**, and never by a coloured
chip, badge or dot. Minimum visible type **14 pt**; principal labels **≥ 18 pt**;
text contrast **≥ 4.5 : 1**; border contrast **≥ 3 : 1**.

## 13. Producer-failure test

**Read every answer area.** If any contains anything other than
`NOT YET ESTABLISHED`, **STOP.**

**Ask a reader to extract one Triviron answer from anywhere on the slide.** If
they can, **STOP.**

**Ask which question group matters most.** If a reader can tell from the layout,
the grid has implied priority and must be redrawn.
