## 1. Purpose and five-second takeaway

**Purpose.** Refuse the zero-clash proxy, carry the nine completion conditions
**with their qualifiers intact**, and bound what a completed cycle does and does
not mean.

**Five-second takeaway.** *Nine conditions. Every box empty. `GCR-006` open.*

**Why this form.** **Nine visibly empty boxes are the only form that states
*these are requirements, not a record*.** Placing the qualifiers above the list
makes them govern every row rather than hang off one. **Styling condition 8 as an
exception would make carrying a matter forward look like failure.**

**Simplification rule.** Condition wording may compress to its operative verb
phrase. **No condition may be deleted, merged or reordered**; **no box may be
ticked, shaded or partially filled**; **the four qualifiers may never be moved
below the list or reduced to a footnote marker**; **the words `required` and
`material` must remain attached to the seven conditions that carry them.**

## 5. Exact visible wording

**Every string below is mandatory on the slide.**

**Qualifiers, printed above the list**

**`for a defined coordination cycle`** · **`as applicable`** · **`required`** ·
**`material`**

**The nine, in order, with their qualifiers**

1. **required** inputs identified
2. **required** readiness checks performed
3. **required** coordination checks performed
4. **material** findings triaged
5. **required** Issues created and assigned
6. **required** resolutions or dispositions recorded
7. **required** verification completed
8. unresolved matters **explicitly carried forward or escalated**
9. coordination evidence retained

**And**

- *"Completion is not 'zero clashes.' A zero-clash report can be produced by
  testing nothing, excluding everything, or resolving symptoms rather than
  interfaces"*
- **`This is not an unconditional universal checklist`**
- **`Carrying a matter forward is a governed outcome, not concealment`**

**Lower strip, all five**

completion does **not** mean the whole design is complete · technically approved ·
construction-ready · accepted · published

**Final status**

**`NOT DEMONSTRATED`** · **`GCR-006 OPEN`** ·
**`None of the nine is recorded as performed`**

## 8. Status and warning treatment

**Mandatory warning: the final status pair `NOT DEMONSTRATED` and `GCR-006
OPEN`**, and the **`This is not an unconditional universal checklist`** line
adjacent to the qualifiers.

**Every box is deliberately empty and visibly a box** — outlined, sized,
present. An absent mark is not the same as an empty box, and only the empty box
says *this is a requirement, not a record*.

**Status is carried by words, never by colour alone**, and never by a coloured
chip, badge or dot. Minimum visible type **14 pt**; principal labels **≥ 18 pt**;
text contrast **≥ 4.5 : 1**; border contrast **≥ 3 : 1**.

## 13. Producer-failure test

**Look for any mark inside any box.** If one exists, **STOP.**

**Ask a reader how many of the nine have been done.** If any number other than
*none is recorded as performed* is given, the status line has failed.

**Cover the qualifier band and ask whether all nine are always required.** If the
answer is *yes*, the band is doing necessary work and **must never be moved**.
