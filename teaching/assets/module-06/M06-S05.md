## 1. Purpose and five-second takeaway

**Purpose.** Establish readiness as the **entry condition** to a cycle, and
separate eight things that a platform view makes look identical.

**Five-second takeaway.** *Visible is not selected. Selected is not ready.
Excluded is a governed outcome.*

**Why this form.** A pipeline implies that inputs proceed and that stopping is
failure. **A gate with two equal exits makes exclusion a governed outcome.** The
equality of the two exit shapes is the mitigation, and it is **structural, not
stylistic**.

**Simplification rule.** Register field names and readiness conditions may
compress to their key noun. **Neither list may lose an entry** — twelve and ten
are counts the source records. **The two exits may never differ in size, border
weight, type size or emphasis**, and **the eight-way comparison may never be
reduced below eight rows.**

## 7. Connector semantics

**Two connectors only**, both from the gate frame's right edge to an exit, **both

**No connector runs from the register list into the gate** — the fields describe

## 8. Status and warning treatment

**Mandatory warning: `Exclusion from one cycle establishes nothing about the
project`**, placed **beside or beneath the excluded exit, inside its visual
group** — not in a footnote — **and** the absence-of-observation line with the
`ARC-01` observation.

**The geometric equality of the two exits is itself the status treatment.** If
one exit is smaller, lighter, lower or red, the slide has made exclusion a
failure.

**Status is carried by words, never by colour alone**, and never by a coloured
chip, badge or dot. Minimum visible type **14 pt**; principal labels **≥ 18 pt**;
text contrast **≥ 4.5 : 1**; border and connector contrast **≥ 3 : 1**.

**Reading order:** register fields → gate label → ten conditions → **both exits,

## 13. Producer-failure test

**Measure the two exits.** If they differ in any dimension, border or type size,
**STOP.**

**Ask a reader what an excluded container says about the project.** If the answer
contains *behind*, *missing*, *late* or *failed*, the exclusion warning has
failed.

**Ask how many of the eight conditions mean "ready".** If the answer is more than
one, the comparison has failed.
