## 1. Purpose and five-second takeaway

Show the two side by side, and that **one does not produce the other**.

**If the audience takes one thing from this visual in five seconds:**

> Being able to move the file establishes nothing at all.

## 2. Source and authority basis

**`CGD-C03` is the controlling condition** and is quoted on the slide: *"each transition requires the applicable governed checks, authorisation and evidence."*

`S2` §2 and §3 supply the anatomy of a transition; `S2` §14 and §17 supply the direction rules. `S1` §6.9 gives **permission is not authority**, and `S4` §9 gives its sharpest form: *"being able to perform an action in the software says nothing about who was authorised to decide it."*

**The left panel's negative list is sourced, not rhetorical.** Upload, move, copy, rename, permission change and new version establish **none** of: checks, evidence, authority, permitted use, suitability, publication, delivery, receipt, acceptance.

Source identifiers `S1`–`S14` are defined in

## 4. Fixed layout

**Two panels, side by side, the same size.**

**Left — `TECHNICAL ACTION`**: six things a user can technically do, then the list of what they establish: **nothing**.

**Right — `GOVERNED TRANSITION`**: a six-step labelled sequence and the `CGD-C03` quotation.

**Between them: nothing but the marker line** — `NO CONNECTOR. The left does not produce the right.`

**A large governance panel beside a small technical one turns an accurate distinction into a lecture.** Equal panels say *these are two different things*, which is the point.

## 6. Geometry or spatial relationships

Reference canvas **960 × 540 pt**, side margins **48 pt**, minimum type **14 pt**.

**Spatial relationships — fixed:**

| Relationship | Rule |
|---|---|
| Panels | **Two, side by side, identical width and identical height** |
| Gutter | **One, centred, and empty** — it carries the marker line and nothing else |
| Left contents | Six actions, then the negative list at the panel foot |
| Right contents | Six labelled steps in a vertical run, then the quotation at the panel foot |
| Marker line | **Beneath the gutter**, centred, spanning both panels |

**Equal panel size is a control.** Set both numerically.

**The gutter must stay visibly empty.** Any graphic placed in it — a bracket, an icon, a dividing chevron — will be read as a relationship.

## 7. Connector semantics

**Not applicable between the panels — no connector, by requirement.**

**No connector, arrow or equals sign between the two panels.** A connector asserts causation — **the exact claim `CGD-C03` refuses.**

**Inside the right panel**, the six steps are joined by **plain downward arrows**, because they *are* a sequence. **Inside the left panel there is no sequence and no connector** — the six actions are a list, not an order.

**No arrow crosses the gutter in either direction.**

## 8. Status and warning treatment

**Governance `CONTROLLED GOVERNANCE` appears**, attached to `CGD-C03` as an active condition. **Implementation is `Not applicable`** and is stated as such rather than left blank.

**The `Teaching structure` label on the right panel is mandatory** — without it the six-step anatomy reads as a controlled procedure rather than a presenter's arrangement.

**The right panel is a labelled sequence, not populated.** Six steps, no fields — **`W10` unpacks one**, and populating them here duplicates that visual and overloads this one.

## 9. Build or reveal sequence

**One frame — both panels appear together.**

**A staged reveal is prohibited.** Revealing the technical panel first and the governance panel second **enacts the causation the slide denies** — the audience sees one become the other.

## 10. Mandatory omissions

**Any connector between the panels** · any equals sign · any causal arrow · any bracket, chevron or icon in the gutter · any permission screenshot · any folder tree · any product logo · any green completion treatment · any populated fields in the right panel.

## 11. Accessibility and projection requirements

- **The absence of a connector is stated in text** — `NO CONNECTOR. The left does not produce the right.` — because an absence cannot be perceived by a screen reader.
- **Both panels use the same border weight and type scale**, so neither reads as the conclusion of the other.
- **No meaning depends on colour**, and no green appears.
- **Reading order** — left panel title, six actions, negative list; right panel title, six steps, quotation; then the marker line.
- **Type: panel titles 20 pt, contents 16 pt, negative list 14 pt minimum, marker line 16 pt.**
- **Contrast ≥ 4.5:1** for text; **≥ 3:1** for panel borders.

## 12. Screen-reader or presenter-notes description

> Two panels of equal size, side by side. **Nothing is drawn between them.** Left panel, Technical action: upload; move; copy; rename; change permissions; create a new version. This establishes **none** of: checks, evidence, authority, permitted use, suitability, publication, delivery, receipt, acceptance. Right panel, Governed transition, labelled *teaching structure*: required input, then checks, then gate conditions, then an authorised decision, then recorded evidence, then changed permitted use. Quotation: each transition requires the applicable governed checks, authorisation and evidence — CDE structure governance decision, condition C03. Beneath: **no connector. The left does not produce the right.**

## 13. Producer-failure test

The visual fails if a viewer can reasonably conclude:

- the technical action **produces** the governed transition;
- the two panels are **two halves of one process**;
- **software capability** establishes authority;
- the six-step anatomy is a **controlled procedure** rather than a teaching arrangement.

## 14. STOP conditions

**Stop production and return to the `W8` specification if:**

- **any connector, arrow, bracket or equals sign appears between the panels** — this is a STOP failure, not a preference;
- an icon or chevron is placed in the gutter;
- the panels become unequal in size;
- the right panel's fields are populated;
- the `Teaching structure` label is dropped;
- a permission screenshot, folder tree or product logo is introduced;
- a green completion treatment appears;
- the slide is animated so that one panel becomes the other.
