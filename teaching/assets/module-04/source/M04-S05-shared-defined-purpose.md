## 1. Purpose and five-second takeaway

Show that Shared carries a **stated purpose**, that reliance is bounded by it, and that **responsibility stays with the originator**.

**If the audience takes one thing from this visual in five seconds:**

> Shared means authorised for a defined use — not approved for every use.

## 2. Source and authority basis

`S1` §6.3 defines Shared as *"information made available beyond the originating task team for an identified purpose, **after required checking and authorisation**"*. `S2` §1 supplies the refusal the slide turns on: *"Shared does not mean published, accepted, or suitable for every purpose."*

`S1` §6.5 and §6.6 carry the responsibility position — **the originating task team retains responsibility**; consumption does not transfer it. `S1` §7.7 records that **no numeric quality thresholds are set**, which is why the checking requirement is not drawn as a checklist.

`S3` §3.3 is the placement caution: *"placement alone does not evidence that checking or authorisation occurred."* `S4` §7 is the implementation limit — **only Architecture is currently demonstrable as a Shared input.**

## 4. Fixed layout

**One central container**, with the originating task team to its left and the purpose call-outs to its right.

**Two or three purpose call-outs**, each terminating in a labelled purpose — **not a fan of arrows radiating outward.**

**The responsibility connection runs from the originator to the container and stays attached.**

Beneath: the on-slide warning.

## 6. Geometry or spatial relationships

Reference canvas **960 × 540 pt**, side margins **48 pt**, minimum type **14 pt**.

**Spatial relationships — fixed:**

| Relationship | Rule |
|---|---|
| Container | **Centred**, occupying roughly the middle third of the content width |
| Originator | **Left of the container**, connected to it |
| Purpose call-outs | **Right of the container**, two or three, **each terminating in a labelled purpose** |
| Responsibility line | **From the originator to the container, drawn as a persistent connection** — a double or heavy line, never an arrow away from the originator |
| Warning | **Beneath the whole arrangement**, full content width |

**The call-outs must look like conditions, not benefits.** A fan of arrows radiating outward reads as distribution; **the same arrows terminating in labelled purposes read as bounded permission.** Terminate every call-out in a label — **none may end in open space.**

## 7. Connector semantics

| Connector | Form | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Originator ↔ container | **Persistent connection — double or heavy line, no arrowhead away from the originator** | **Responsibility is retained**, not handed over |
| Container → each purpose | **Plain line terminating in a labelled purpose** | Bounded permission for a stated use |
| Container → anywhere else | **Prohibited — draw none** | There is no handover, no publication and no acceptance on this slide |

**No handover arrow.** An arrow leaving the originator and arriving at the container asserts a transfer of responsibility that `S1` §6.5 refuses.

## 8. Status and warning treatment

**Governance `CONTROLLED GOVERNANCE` and implementation `IMPLEMENTATION UNVERIFIED` both appear**, with the implementation status carrying its qualifier — **only Architecture currently demonstrable as a Shared input.**

**The warning is on the slide**, beneath the arrangement, at body size.

**No approval stamp, publication symbol, acceptance symbol or tick.** **No green** — green reads as *cleared*, which is the misreading the slide exists to refuse.

## 9. Build or reveal sequence

**One frame preferred.** If built, the permitted order is: **container**, then **originator and its retained-responsibility connection**, then **the purpose call-outs**, then **the warning**.

**The warning may not be the last-revealed element if the slide might be cut short.** If the deck animates, the warning appears with the container.

## 10. Mandatory omissions

Any approval stamp · any publication symbol · any client-acceptance symbol · any tick · **any green compliance treatment** · any platform screenshot · any handover arrow · any invented example purpose · any suggestion that placement in `02. Shared` evidences checking or authorisation.

## 11. Accessibility and projection requirements

- **The retained-responsibility relationship is stated in words** — `RESPONSIBILITY RETAINED` — not carried by line weight alone.
- **Purpose call-outs are labelled text**, so the boundedness survives a viewer who cannot resolve the line terminations.
- **No meaning depends on colour**, and **no green is used at all**.
- **Reading order** — originator and its responsibility label, container and its stated-purpose line, each purpose call-out in turn, then the warning.
- **Type: container title 20 pt, contents 16 pt, call-out labels 16 pt, warning 18 pt, status labels 14 pt minimum.**
- **Contrast ≥ 4.5:1** for text; **≥ 3:1** for the container border and connectors.

## 12. Screen-reader or presenter-notes description

> A central container titled Shared Container, carrying a stated-purpose line that is **left unfilled**. To its left, the originating task team, connected by a persistent line labelled responsibility retained — **not an arrow away from the originator**. To its right, three call-outs, each terminating in a labelled purpose: coordination; controlled review; reference by another task team. Beneath: Shared means authorised for a defined use. It does not mean approved for every use. **There is no approval mark, publication symbol or acceptance symbol anywhere on this visual.**

## 13. Producer-failure test

The visual fails if a viewer can reasonably conclude:

- Shared means **approved**, **signed off** or **cleared for any use**;
- responsibility **transferred** to the receiving team;
- Shared is **Published**;
- placement in `02. Shared` **evidences** that checking or authorisation occurred.

## 14. STOP conditions

**Stop production and return to the `W5` specification if:**

- an approval stamp, publication symbol, acceptance symbol or tick appears;
- **green is used anywhere** on the visual;
- the responsibility line becomes a handover arrow;
- a purpose call-out terminates in open space rather than a label;
- an example purpose is invented and printed on the stated-purpose line;
- the warning is removed or moved to the notes;
- a platform screenshot or folder tree is introduced.

## 15. Native PowerPoint reconstruction notes

**Reconstruct with native PowerPoint shapes, tables and text boxes.** **No image
is imported, and no diagram-rendering engine is used.**

**One rectangle, one marker for the originator, three call-out lines, two text boxes.**

1. Container as a plain rectangle, centred. **Leave the stated-purpose line visibly blank** — an underscore run or an empty ruled line, **not a grey placeholder word.**
2. Originator as a labelled shape to the left. Connect with a **double line or a 3 pt plain connector, arrowheads set to none at both ends.**
3. Three straight connectors to the right, each **terminating at a text label**. Do not use a radial or "diverging arrows" SmartArt layout.
4. **Use no green fill and no tick glyph anywhere on the slide**, including in the theme's accent colours if they are applied automatically.
5. Warning as its own text box beneath, 18 pt.
6. If animating, group the container and the warning so they enter together.
