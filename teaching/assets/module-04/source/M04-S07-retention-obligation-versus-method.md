## 1. Purpose and five-second takeaway

Show retention as an **established obligation** with an **undecided method**.

**If the audience takes one thing from this visual in five seconds:**

> The obligation to retain is settled. How to do it is not — and it is not a folder.

## 2. Source and authority basis

`S1` §6.3 defines Record / Retained as *"historical evidence retained for traceability, according to the project's retention approach"* — **and the approach is what is undecided.** `S1` §7.10, §9.9 and §12.10 carry the obligation: preserve required information, protect against uncontrolled change or loss, maintain traceability, retain required evidence.

**`CGD-C06` is the controlling condition:** **no mandatory `04 Archive` root is approved or required.** `S3` §3.1 records that `0. Common Files` maps to no state; the state register records that **Record / Retained has no approved area**. `S2` §19 lists the retention method among the unresolved matters.

**`superseded ≠ deleted`** is the obligation panel's own distinction, and it is what keeps the panel meaningful without a method.

## 4. Fixed layout

**Two panels, side by side, the same size.**

**Left — `OBLIGATION — ESTABLISHED`**: four obligation lines and the `superseded ≠ deleted` distinction.

**Right — `METHOD — UNRESOLVED`**: four fields, **all unfilled**, and a `← stays empty →` marker.

Beneath both: the on-slide statement, then the state-versus-area mismatch, **both directions**.

**A large obligation panel beside a small method panel implies the method is a detail.** It is an undecided governance matter, and **the panels must look equally consequential.**

## 8. Status and warning treatment

**Governance is split and both halves appear** — the obligation is `CONTROLLED GOVERNANCE`, the method is `UNRESOLVED`. **Implementation is `Not addressed in any validation record`**, which is different from *unverified* and is stated as such.

**The method panel appears and stays empty.** **An omitted method panel reads as complete**; an empty one shows the open decision.

**No completed storage icon, no green check, no archive imagery.** **No invented retention period** — none exists in any controlled source. **No invented named holder** — no records manager or archive administrator exists in the Harrismith set.

## 9. Build or reveal sequence

**One frame — both panels appear together.**

**A staged reveal is prohibited.** Revealing the obligation panel first makes the empty method panel a punchline, and a frame showing only an established obligation implies retention is in hand.

## 10. Mandatory omissions

**`04 Archive` in any form** — not as a label, an example, a grey placeholder, a future suggestion or a visual destination · any folder icon · any placeholder folder name · any destination arrow · any completed storage icon · any green check · any archive imagery · **any invented retention period** · **any invented named holder** · any four-state-to-four-area alignment.

## 11. Accessibility and projection requirements

- **The method panel's emptiness is carried in words** — `TBD`, `unavailable`, `stays empty` — not by whitespace alone, which a screen reader would skip entirely.
- **No meaning depends on colour.** The unresolved panel is **not** shaded, hatched or greyed; shading reads as failure.
- **Both panels use the same border weight and type scale**, so neither reads as subordinate.
- **Reading order** — obligation title and its five lines, method title and its five lines, the statement, then the two mismatch lines.
- **Type: panel titles 20 pt, contents 16 pt, mismatch lines 14 pt minimum, statement 18 pt.**
- **Contrast ≥ 4.5:1** for text; **≥ 3:1** for panel borders.

## 12. Screen-reader or presenter-notes description

> Two panels of equal size, side by side, **with nothing drawn between them**. Left panel, Obligation — established: preserve required project information; protect against uncontrolled change or loss; maintain traceability; retain required evidence; superseded is not the same as deleted. Right panel, Method — unresolved: location, TBD; retention period, TBD; responsible holder, TBD; implementation evidence, unavailable. The panel is marked **stays empty**. Beneath: Record / Retained is a state or obligation. It is **not** automatically a folder. Also: 0. Common Files is an area mapped to no state; Record / Retained is a state mapped to no approved area. **No folder, archive or storage location appears anywhere on this visual.**

## 13. Producer-failure test

The visual fails if a viewer can reasonably conclude:

- Record / Retained **is a folder**, or will be one;
- an **`04 Archive` root exists**, is proposed, or is coming;
- a **retention period or holder** has been decided;
- the obligation panel **produces** the method;
- the four areas and four states **align**.

## 14. STOP conditions

**Stop production and return to the `W7` specification if:**

- **`04 Archive` appears anywhere**, in any form, including as a greyed suggestion;
- a folder icon, storage icon or archive graphic is introduced;
- the method panel is **filled, shaded, greyed or removed**;
- a retention period, date or named holder is invented;
- a destination arrow is drawn from the obligation panel;
- the panels become unequal in size;
- the mismatch is redrawn as a four-area-to-four-state alignment;
- a green check appears.

## 15. Native PowerPoint reconstruction notes

**Reconstruct with native PowerPoint shapes, tables and text boxes.** **No image
is imported, and no diagram-rendering engine is used.**

**Two rectangles of identical size, three text boxes. No icons.**

1. Draw both panels and **set width and height numerically to the same values.** Do not size the second by dragging.
2. Method panel contents as a **two-column text box or a borderless 4 × 2 table** — labels left, `TBD` right. **Keep the values visible as text**; do not leave the right column blank.
3. **Apply no fill and no shading to the method panel.** If the theme fills shapes by default, clear it — a shaded panel reads as a failure state.
4. **Insert no icon of any kind.** PowerPoint's icon library contains folder, archive and box graphics, and every one of them would assert a location this project has not decided.
5. Statement and mismatch as text boxes beneath, full width.
6. **No entrance animation** — both panels appear together.
