## 1. Purpose and five-second takeaway

**Purpose.** Teach the three Domain 3 translation questions — `TDR-007`–
`TDR-009`.

**Five-second takeaway.** *Federation is a lens. It moves no authorship — and
nothing here says what Triviron federates.*

**Why this form.** Family **F.1**. **Slide-specific:** `TDR-009`'s `METHOD`
classification is the module's first appearance of **a classification and a
status that read as if they disagree**. The three-card form with a two-line
zone 4 makes `METHOD` + `NOT YET ESTABLISHED` legible as **two independent facts
about one row**, which is exactly rule **C5**.

**Simplification rule (family F.8, plus slide-specific).** **The `METHOD`
qualifier `reusable structural distinction — not evidence of Triviron adoption`
may never be dropped, shortened or moved to a footnote.**
