## 1. Purpose and five-second takeaway

**Purpose.** Close Module 7 by showing what future governed work must accomplish
before a project-specific Triviron BEP could responsibly be populated, and bridge
to Module 8.

**Five-second takeaway.** *The answers have to be earned, and nothing here says
when.*

**Why this form.** The accepted workflow has seven stages and **one of them is an
authority engagement that may produce nothing**. Drawing seven boxes in a run
would make the workflow look like a process that completes. Splitting it at the
authority gate makes the conditionality structural. **The header band is the
second control:** without an explicit `TEACHING WORKFLOW` declaration, a
seven-stage diagram on a closing slide reads as the project's plan (`M7-S11`,
`M7V-S30`).

**Simplification rule.** Stage labels may compress to three or four words. **No
stage may be deleted, reordered, merged or moved across the gate strip.** **The
header declaration, the gate strip, the complete method one-liner and the
boundary strip may never be shortened or reduced below 18 pt.** **The method
one-liner appears in full, all nine objects, or not at all** — a partial chain
would omit the STOP. **The header declaration's second semantic statement may
wrap onto a second physical line, and the semicolon is the only permitted break
point — a layout break only, altering no wording.** If space is short, compress
stage labels and roadmap segment text.

**If the native build cannot reproduce the header wording at ≥ 18 pt within
`y = 84–148`, the producer STOPS and refers back — type is never silently
shrunk, condensed or letter-spaced to force a fit** (rule **G8**).

## 10. Typography, status and typed-absence treatment

**Minimum visible type 14 pt · principal labels ≥ 18 pt · every header, gate,
one-liner and boundary word ≥ 18 pt · contrast ≥ 4.5 : 1 · border and connector
contrast ≥ 3 : 1.**

**Header declaration typography — mandatory:** single (1.0) line spacing · no
paragraph spacing before or after · **no font condensation** · **no reduced
character spacing used merely to force fit** · internal top and bottom margins
small enough for three 18 pt lines to sit cleanly inside the 64 pt band.

**Mandatory warning:** the header declaration's two lines and the gate strip's
two lines, **each pair together**, plus the boundary strip in full.

**Typed absence.** **Register 2 is not styled as unreached, blocked, greyed or
pending** — it is a later part of a teaching workflow. **The `only when earned`
condition is a stated condition inside its own box**, never a lock, a barrier, a
padlock or a greyed state. **The handed-forward zeros are stated neutrally and
are not drawn as work the workshop will erase.** **`NONE IDENTIFIED` is not
`NONE EXISTS`.**

**Status meaning is carried by words, never by colour alone.**
