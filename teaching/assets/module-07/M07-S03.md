## 1. Purpose and five-second takeaway

**Purpose.** Teach the accepted analytical sequence and the STOP rule.

**Five-second takeaway.** *The chain stops in the middle, and that is the current
state.*

**Why this form.** The chain has nine objects and **one of them is the STOP**.
Drawing it as a single nine-box run would make the STOP one box among nine and
would carry the reader straight past it. Splitting the run and giving the STOP
the full width makes it **structurally impossible to read the chain as
continuous** — which is exactly the accepted teaching position: *the chain
currently stops before candidate decisions, by rule and by evidence*. `M7V-S17`
forbids the STOP as an annotation; this form makes it the largest object instead.

**Simplification rule.** Step labels may compress to two or three words. **No
step may be deleted, reordered, merged or moved across the STOP band.** **The
STOP band's wording may never be shortened, reduced below 18 pt, or moved.** **No
count in the current-position panel may be summarised, rounded or dropped** —
the five figures appear together or not at all. If space is short, **compress
step labels, never the STOP band or the panel.**

## 9. Connector semantics and prohibitions

**Six connectors exist, and only six** — three between horizontally adjacent
boxes within the upper row, three within the lower row.

**Each means exactly:** **`the next step in the analytical sequence — the order
in which the translation problem is worked`**. **Nothing moves along them; no
Harrismith value becomes a Triviron answer by traversing them.**

**Prohibited without exception:**

- **No connector crosses, enters, leaves or passes behind the STOP band**
  (`M7V-S16`).
- **The upper row's last step does not connect to the lower row's first step** —
  that connector is exactly the automatic evidence-to-decision progression rule
  **K6** forbids.
- **No connector enters or leaves the current-position panel.**
- **No connector loops back.** No closed cycle.

## 10. Typography, status and typed-absence treatment

**Minimum visible type 14 pt · principal labels ≥ 18 pt · every STOP-band and
panel status word ≥ 18 pt · text contrast ≥ 4.5 : 1 · border and connector
contrast ≥ 3 : 1.**

**Mandatory warning: the three STOP-band lines together**, and
**`NONE IDENTIFIED ≠ NONE EXISTS` inside the current-position panel's own
frame** (rule **G7** — not in a slide footer).

**Typed absence.** The five zeros and `NONE IDENTIFIED × 21` are stated as **the
controlled evidence position**, in normal weight, inside a normally bordered
panel. **Reaching the STOP boundary is not failure and must not be drawn as
one** — no red, no alarm, no barrier-tape styling, no "blocked" iconography, no
unticked boxes, no empty progress toward the lower row. The lower four steps
carry **`not reached`**, never `incomplete`, `pending`, `overdue` or `0%`.

**Status meaning is carried by words, never by colour alone.**

## 17. Producer-failure test

**Accepted semantic test, carried unweakened from `M7V-03`:**

> **Ask a reader to trace the route from step 1 to step 8 with a finger.** If the
> finger crosses the STOP band without stopping, the band has failed. **Then ask:
> what do the arrows carry?** If any content, value or answer is named, **STOP**.
> **Then ask: is the lower row a problem?** If *yes*, the equal-styling rule has
> failed and the visual must be redrawn.

**Source-reconstruction test (added by `T7-K`, semantic test unaltered):** count
connectors — the count must be exactly **six**, all within a row. Confirm the
STOP band is the tallest and heaviest object. If either fails, **STOP and refer
back**.
