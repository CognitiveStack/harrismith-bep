## 1. Purpose and five-second takeaway

**Purpose.** Teach the precise typed-absence vocabulary and why disciplined
absence is a positive information-management result rather than a blank to be
guessed away.

**Five-second takeaway.** *Four kinds of unknown. The reason decides which one.*

**Why this form.** **Quadrants have no first and no last.** A row of four would
be read left to right as a progression, and a column of four as a descent — both
would breach `M7-S13` and `M7V-S22`, and both contradict the accepted teaching
position that **the four terms are semantic states, not stages**. The separating
rule below the quadrants carries the second, easily missed distinction:
**`NONE IDENTIFIED` is an evidence-availability position, not a field-18
status** (rule **A7**), and putting it in a fifth quadrant would merge two
registers of absence.

**Simplification rule.** Each quadrant's meaning may compress to its operative
clause, but **each quadrant must retain both its meaning and its misuse bar** —
the bar is what prevents substitution. **The separating rule, the
evidence-position panel and the misuse-warning strip may never be shortened,
merged or reduced below 18 pt.** If space is short, **compress the meanings —
never the bars.**
