## 1. Purpose and five-second takeaway

**Purpose.** Teach the three Domain 7 translation questions — `TDR-019`–
`TDR-021`.

**Five-second takeaway.** *Ability to move a file is not authority to change its
state — and nobody here holds that authority.*

**Why this form.** The four acts — completion, publication, exchange, recipient
acceptance — are the row's reusable distinction, and **the distinction is the
separation itself**. Four adjacent cells with no connectors carry it; four cells
with arrows would assert a sequence, and a sequence would assert an adopted
Triviron exchange workflow (`M7-S02`, `M7V-S18`).

**Simplification rule (family F.8, plus slide-specific).** **None of the four act
cells may be dropped, merged, renamed or reordered**, and **the `METHOD`
qualifier on `TDR-021` may never be dropped or shortened.**

## 17. Producer-failure test

**Accepted family test F.13, carried unweakened.**

**Accepted slide-specific test, carried unweakened from `M7V-11`:**

> **Ask a reader who may authorise publication for Triviron.** If any answer is
> offered, **STOP**. **Then ask whether Triviron has a publication authority.**
> If the answer is *no*, the slide has failed in the second direction — the only
> correct answer is *this programme has not identified one, which says nothing
> about whether one exists*. **Then check for any arrow between the four acts.**
> If one exists, **STOP**.

measure the four act cells. If they are not exactly 61 pt each with 4 pt gaps,
or if the residual has been rounded into a cell rather than distributed to the
insets, **STOP and refer back**.
