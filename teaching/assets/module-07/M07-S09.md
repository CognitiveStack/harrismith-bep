## 1. Purpose and five-second takeaway

**Purpose.** Teach the three Domain 5 translation questions — `TDR-013`–
`TDR-015`.

**Five-second takeaway.** *Detecting something does not decide what it becomes —
and no vocabulary here is Triviron's.*

**Why this form.** Family **F.1**. **Slide-specific:** the accepted teaching
sequence *detect → classify → decide whether to create a governed record → apply
governed status meanings* is **a teaching sequence, not a claim about what each
stage inherently is**. The co-equal card row refuses to draw it, which prevents
the sequence from being read as an adopted workflow.

**Simplification rule (family F.8, plus slide-specific).** **The `METHOD`
qualifier on `TDR-013` may never be dropped or shortened**, and **neither
Harrismith Issue definition may be quoted on this slide.**
