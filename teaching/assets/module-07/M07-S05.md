## 1. Purpose and five-second takeaway

**Purpose.** Teach the three Domain 1 translation questions — `TDR-001`–
`TDR-003`.

**Five-second takeaway.** *Coordination has to be governed before any tool
matters — and none of it is settled here.*

**Why this form (family F.1, plus slide-specific).** Three co-equal cards, one
per accepted decision question. **Slide-specific:** the three questions are
**sequentially dependent in argument** — purpose, then governing document, then
cycle — and the co-equal card row deliberately refuses to draw that as a flow,
because the dependency is analytical and **none of the three is nearer to an
answer than the others**.

**Simplification rule (family F.8).** Zone 2 evidence-kind lists may compress to
short noun phrases. **Zone 1's question, zone 3's two lines and zone 4's two
labelled lines may never be shortened, merged, moved or reduced below 18 pt for
the status and classification words.** **The exclusion strip and the boundary
strip may never be shortened.** If space is short, **cut zone 2 detail — never
zone 1, 3, 4 or either strip.**

## 4. Fixed layout and object hierarchy — family F.1, F.2, F.3

**Three co-equal cards in one row, one per accepted decision question, each
carrying four stacked zones; beneath the cards a full-width exclusion strip;
beneath that a full-width boundary strip.**

| Rank | Object | Rule |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | The three cards | **Strictly co-equal** in width, height, border, fill and type. No card may be emphasised, widened, ordered first by weight, or styled as more advanced, more important or nearer to an answer |
| 2 | The exclusion strip | **A principal object, not a footnote** |
| 3 | The boundary strip | **A principal object, not a footnote** |

**The four card zones (family F.3):**

| Zone | Holds | Rule |
|---:|---|---|
| **Z1** | `TDR-` identifier + the accepted decision question, verbatim | **Phrased as a matter to decide, never as an answer.** No card may rephrase a question into a statement |
| **Z2** | The **kinds** of controlled Triviron source that would be needed (register field 7) | **Kinds only.** Never a guessed content, named document, named platform or named organisation |

| **Z4** | **`classification:`** *value* and **`status: NOT YET ESTABLISHED`** | **Two independently labelled lines** |

**No card may contain a fabricated Triviron answer.**

## 9. Connector semantics and prohibitions — family F.7

**Zero connectors on this slide.**

No arrow between cards · between zones · from a card to either strip · into or
out of any card.

**An arrow between two cards would assert an order among the three questions
that the register does not state**, and an arrow from zone 2 to zone 4 would
assert automatic progression from evidence to a decision — rule **K6**,
`M7V-S18`.

**Slide-specific:** **no arrow from `TDR-001` to `TDR-002` to `TDR-003`**, even
though the argument runs in that order. **The order is spoken, never drawn.**

**The relationship between the three cards is co-equality, carried by identical
geometry and stated in words.** **Zero means zero.**

## 10. Typography, status and typed-absence treatment — family F.9

**Minimum visible type 14 pt · principal labels ≥ 18 pt · every classification,
status, exclusion and boundary word ≥ 18 pt · text contrast ≥ 4.5 : 1 · border
contrast ≥ 3 : 1.**

**Mandatory warning:** the boundary strip in full, plus the recorded-variance
line.

**Typed absence.** `NONE IDENTIFIED` and `NOT YET ESTABLISHED` appear on **every
card**, identically, neutrally, in normal weight.

**Prohibited on any card:** grey fill · dashed border · reduced opacity ·
strike-through · cross · unticked box · empty bar · red or amber treatment ·
"0 of 3" tallies · "awaiting" or "pending" wording · any styling that
distinguishes an unanswered card from an answered one — **because there are no
answered cards, and the identical treatment is the point.**

**`TBD` is never applied to a Triviron matter on this slide.**

**Status meaning is carried by words, never by colour alone.**
