## 1. Purpose and five-second takeaway

**Purpose.** Teach the three Domain 6 translation questions — `TDR-016`–
`TDR-018`.

**Five-second takeaway.** *A complete cycle is demonstrated, never asserted — and
the only worked evidence here is Harrismith's.*

**Why this form.** The accepted content places a **Harrismith evidence
illustration** on this slide, and an unlabelled evidence note beside three
Triviron cards is the fastest possible route to a reader taking it as Triviron's
position. **The fence is the mitigation**: its own border, its own attribution
heading, its own separation, and **no connector to anything**.

**Simplification rule (family F.8, plus slide-specific).** **The fence's
attribution heading may never be dropped, shortened, moved outside the fence or
reduced below 18 pt.** If space is short, **cut the evidence detail inside the
fence — never the attribution.**
