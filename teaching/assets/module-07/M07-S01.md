## 1. Purpose and five-second takeaway

**Purpose.** Locate Module 7 in the teaching roadmap and establish the
transition from worked reference implementation to translation method.

**Five-second takeaway.** *We have moved from a worked example to the questions
a future BEP needs.*

**Why this form.** A band expresses position in a sequence of **teaching
modules** without asserting anything about time, project phase or commitment. A
timeline would import dates; a funnel would import progress; an arrow-to-a-goal
would import a project appointment. `M7-S10` and `M7-S11` are breached most
easily on the opening slide, before any boundary has been taught.

**Simplification rule.** The Modules 1–6 subject list may compress to six short
phrases or a single line naming the six subjects. **The framing line and the
boundary strip may never be shortened, reduced below 18 pt, moved into a corner
or split.** If space is short, **cut the Modules 1–6 subject detail — never the
boundary text.**

≥ 3 : 1.**

strip is a **principal visual object** — not small print, not a footer, not a

**Status meaning is carried by words, never by colour alone**, and never by a
