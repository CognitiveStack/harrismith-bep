## 1. Purpose and five-second takeaway

**Purpose.** Teach the three Domain 4 translation questions — `TDR-010`–
`TDR-012`.

**Five-second takeaway.** *No check, no rule, no tolerance and no approver is
inherited — not even as a starting point.*

**Why this form.** This is the slide where a producer is most tempted to supply
an example. **A single illustrative clearance, a sample check row or a "typical"
tolerance would breach `M7-S05` and `M7-S06` instantly and irreversibly.** The
enlarged exclusion block states the exclusions **as content**, so the slide's
most concrete object is the list of what may not appear.

**Simplification rule (family F.8, plus slide-specific).** **No item in the
exclusion block may be dropped, abbreviated into "etc.", or summarised into a
count.** If space is short, **compress zone 2 — never the exclusion block.**
