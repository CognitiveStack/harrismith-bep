## 1. Purpose and five-second takeaway

**Purpose.** Introduce the Translation Decision Register as the working record of
Module 7.

**Five-second takeaway.** *Twenty-one questions. No answers, and none claimed.*

**Why this form.** The backlog's shape — **seven domains × three questions =
21** — is the teaching point, and a grid is the only form that shows the shape
and the count in one view. The classification strip is placed **beneath and
separate from** the grid so that no reader maps a cell to a stage. **The
register's own reconciliation is the only source of every number shown.**

**Simplification rule.** Each of the 21 cells carries its **`TDR-` identifier
plus a short topic label of three to six words**; the full question text belongs
in presenter notes. **No cell may be left blank, merged, spanned or omitted, and
the grid may not be reduced to a count** — the shape is the teaching point.
**The five classification counts appear together or not at all.** **The
`enumeration order — not progression` label and the current-state panel may never
be shortened or reduced below 18 pt.**

## 9. Connector semantics and prohibitions

**No connectors anywhere on the slide.**

**Prohibited without exception:** any arrow between grid and strip · between
strip cells · from a domain row to a classification cell · into or out of the
panel.

**An arrow between two classification cells would create the maturity ladder
`M7V-S21` forbids**, and an arrow from a grid cell to a classification cell
would assert a row's trajectory.

## 10. Typography, status and typed-absence treatment

**Minimum visible type 14 pt · principal labels ≥ 18 pt · every classification,
status and boundary word ≥ 18 pt · text contrast ≥ 4.5 : 1 · border contrast
≥ 3 : 1.** Cells are 40 pt tall; a `TDR-` identifier and a short topic label sit
within that at ≥ 14 pt.

**Mandatory warning:** the strip's `enumeration order, not progression` label
(**inside the strip frame**), the three `prerequisite evidence not identified`
qualifiers **on their own zero cells**, and all six panel entries **together**.

**Typed absence — the three zero-valued classification cells are the
highest-risk objects on the slide.** They are identical in size, border, fill and
type to the two in-use cells, differing **only** in their figure and qualifier.

**Prohibited on any zero cell:** grey fill · dashed border · reduced opacity ·
strike-through · a cross · an unticked box · an empty bar · a `0%` reading ·
placement at the end of a ramp.

**The zeros mean `prerequisite evidence not identified`, never `not yet
achieved`.** The `21` and the `0` must never appear as numerator and denominator
of one another. **`NONE IDENTIFIED` is not `NONE EXISTS`.**

**Status meaning is carried by words, never by colour alone.**

## 17. Producer-failure test

**Accepted semantic test, carried unweakened from `M7V-04`:**

> **Point at the `ESTABLISHED 0` cell and ask a reader what it means.** If the
> answer is *not there yet*, *the goal*, or *the last stage*, the strip has
> failed. **Then ask whether the grid is finished.** If the answer implies the
> cells are waiting to be filled with answers, **STOP** — the grid records
> questions, and a completed grid would still hold questions.

the five strip cells side by side and confirm they are pixel-identical but for
their text. If any zero cell differs in fill, border, opacity or size, **STOP and
refer back**.
