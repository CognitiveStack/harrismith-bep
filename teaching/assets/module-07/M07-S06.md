## 1. Purpose and five-second takeaway

**Purpose.** Teach the three Domain 2 translation questions — `TDR-004`–
`TDR-006`.

**Five-second takeaway.** *Three allocation decisions. Not one holder is known,
and none may be guessed.*

**Why this form.** **This is the module's highest org-chart and holder risk.**
Three questions beginning *"who"* pull a producer toward boxes-and-lines. The
**function/holder split** answers the *"who"* with a **function name and a typed
absence on the same card**, which removes the reason to draw a chart. **No
hierarchy, no reporting line, no tree, no swimlane, no seat, no silhouette, no
avatar** (`M7V-S02`, `M7V-S03`).

**Simplification rule (family F.8, plus slide-specific).** Zone 2 lists may
compress. **The `holder: NOT YET ESTABLISHED` line may never be dropped,
abbreviated, merged into the function line or moved to a single slide-level
note** — it must appear on **each** card, because a single note can be read past.

## 10. Typography, status and typed-absence treatment — family F.9

**Minimum visible type 14 pt · principal labels ≥ 18 pt · every classification,
status, holder, exclusion and boundary word ≥ 18 pt · contrast ≥ 4.5 : 1.**

**Mandatory warning:** the boundary strip, plus **`holder: NOT YET ESTABLISHED`
on all three cards together**, plus the mandatory holder statement.

**Typed absence — slide-specific.** **`holder: NOT YET ESTABLISHED` must not be
drawn as an empty seat, a blank box, a dotted outline, a silhouette, a
question-mark figure, a "vacant" marker or a slot awaiting a name.** It is a
**typed position stated in words**, in the same weight as the function line.

**Family prohibitions apply on every card:** no grey, dashes, reduced opacity,
strike-through, cross, unticked box, empty bar, red or amber, "0 of 3" tallies,
"awaiting" or "pending" wording. **`TBD` is never applied to a Triviron matter.**
