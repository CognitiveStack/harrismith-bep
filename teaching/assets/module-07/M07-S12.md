## 1. Purpose and five-second takeaway

**Purpose.** Teach how to state the current translation position precisely
without overstating evidence or governance maturity.

**Five-second takeaway.** *Five kinds of statement. Not five rungs.*

**Why this form.** **A strip, a row, a staircase or a grid all supply an order
the schema denies.** The offset arrangement makes it impossible to read a first,
a last, a rising line or a column, and the hub sits at the centre of the
arrangement rather than at one end — so **no card is closer to a goal**.
`M7V-S21` is the governing control, and **layout is the only reliable
mitigation**: a caption under a left-to-right strip would be read past.

**Simplification rule.** Each card's semantic boundary may compress to its
operative clause. **No card may be dropped, resized, merged or moved into
alignment with another.** **The five counts appear together or not at all.**
**The hub's two lines and the foot strip may never be shortened or reduced below
18 pt.** If space is short, **compress the boundary clauses — never the counts,
the hub or the foot strip.**

## 9. Connector semantics and prohibitions

**No connectors anywhere.**

**Prohibited without exception:** any arrow between cards · **any spoke from a
card to the hub** · **any ring, orbit, circle or wheel joining the cards**.

**A spoke would make the hub a parent and the five cards its children; a ring
would make them a cycle.**

**The hub's relationship to the cards is carried by central position and by its
own words**, and **that must be stated in the alt text**.

## 10. Typography, status and typed-absence treatment

**Minimum visible type 14 pt · principal labels ≥ 18 pt · every classification
word, count, qualifier, hub line and foot-strip word ≥ 18 pt · contrast
≥ 4.5 : 1.**

**Mandatory warning:** the hub's two lines **together**, and the three
`prerequisite not satisfied` qualifiers **on their own cards**.

**Typed absence — the three zero cards are the highest-risk objects on the
slide.** They are identical to the two in-use cards in size, border, fill and
type.

**Prohibited on any zero card:** grey · dashes · reduced opacity · an
outline-only treatment · a "locked" or "unavailable" reading · placement at one
end · a smaller size.

**The qualifier `not a stage awaiting arrival` is mandatory on each**, because
*zero* on a category otherwise reads as *not yet reached*. **`NONE IDENTIFIED`
is not `NONE EXISTS`.**

**Status meaning is carried by words, never by colour alone.**

## 11. Source, authority and teaching-synthesis labels

**`TEACHING SYNTHESIS`** — the third foot-strip entry (*precision is progress —
say exactly what the evidence permits, no more and no less.*) is **not a quoted
source sentence**. It carries the words `TEACHING SYNTHESIS` on the slide, is
visually distinguishable from quoted schema and register wording, and the
distinction is carried **by that word or a separate labelled zone — never by
colour or italics alone**. It is never drawn as governance, as a register value
or as a quotation.

**Every count is a quoted register value.** No count may be recalculated,
adjusted or supplemented on the slide.

## 17. Producer-failure test

**Accepted semantic test, carried unweakened from `M7V-12`:**

> **Ask a reader to put the five categories in order.** If they can, and believe
> the order means progress, **the layout has failed and the visual must be
> redrawn.** **Then ask what `ESTABLISHED` means here.** If the answer merges it
> with the status vocabulary's `ESTABLISHED`, the hub's separation line has
> failed. **Then ask whether the zeros are a problem.** If *yes* or *unclear*,
> the qualifiers have failed.

**Source-reconstruction test (added by `T7-K`, semantic test unaltered):** place
the five cards side by side and confirm they are pixel-identical but for their
text. Then confirm no lower card shares a column with an upper card. If either
fails, **STOP and refer back**.
