## 1. Purpose and five-second takeaway

**Purpose.** Establish the governing boundary before any domain content is
taught.

**Five-second takeaway.** *Two lists. No bridge between them.*

**Why this form.** The slide's entire argument is the **absence** between the
columns. Equal columns make the boundary structural rather than asserted; an
empty channel makes the absence visible; a full-width strip beneath both states
that the boundary governs **everything above it**, which a corner caption cannot
do. **A single arrow between the columns would breach `M7-S15`, `M7V-S13` and
the `source-map.md` §1 rule that no arrow may mean `HARRISMITH VALUE → TRIVIRON
VALUE`.**

**Simplification rule.** Either column's items may compress to short noun
phrases. **Items may not be reordered to pair across the columns, and the two
columns need not hold the same number of items** — an enforced pairing would
create the implied connectors the slide exists to prevent. **The boundary strip
may never be shortened or reduced below 18 pt.** If space is short, **cut items,
never strip text.**

## 9. Connector semantics and prohibitions

**ZERO connectors between the two principal columns — mandatory.**
**Zero connectors anywhere on the slide.**

**Prohibited without exception:** arrow · bridge · line · bracket · shared
border · row alignment implying pairing · numbering implying correspondence ·
"vs" symbol · scales or balance imagery · any shape occupying the channel.

**The relationship between the columns is carried entirely by equal weight,

`M7V-S13` and rule **K3** govern. **Zero means zero.**

## 10. Typography, status and typed-absence treatment

**Minimum visible type 14 pt · principal labels ≥ 18 pt · every boundary-strip
word ≥ 18 pt · text contrast ≥ 4.5 : 1 · border contrast ≥ 3 : 1.**

**Mandatory warning: the four boundary-strip entries, all four, together**, plus
the explicit statement that **the channel is empty by rule**.

**Typed absence.** The empty channel must not be styled as a gap awaiting a
bridge — **no dotted line, no faded arrow, no "to be determined" marker, no
ellipsis, no question mark**. The right column's items are **excluded content**,

cross. **`NONE IDENTIFIED` is not `NONE EXISTS`.**

**Status meaning is carried by words, never by colour alone.**
