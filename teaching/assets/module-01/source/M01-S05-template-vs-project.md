## 1. Why this is not a diagram

A flowchart would impose a false relationship — that a template *becomes* a
project BEP through some defined process. No such process is recorded anywhere,
and the distinction is a contrast, not a transformation.

**A two-column table is the honest form.** Two things side by side, neither
flowing into the other.

## 3. Content — five rows per column

The presentation outline holds nine categories per column. **Five is the slide
limit**; the remainder belong in speech.

| A reusable template or framework | A project-specific BEP |
|---|---|
| Standard headings and section architecture | The project's purpose and scope, stated |
| Standard role *categories* | The participating organisations and functions, identified |
| Common naming principles | The information responsibilities, allocated |
| Standard CDE concepts | The required information deliverables, defined |
| Standard review prompts | The delivery events or gates, agreed |

**Central message, on the slide:**

> A template asks the right questions; a project-specific BEP records the
> project's agreed answers.

*(Teaching wording.)*

**Second message, and the harder one:**

> A populated document is not necessarily a complete one. Every heading can
> contain text while the project's decisions remain unmade.

## 4. Unresolved panel — four items, not nine

Beneath the comparison. **This is where the slide earns its place**, because it
shows Harrismith cutting both ways: fully populated, and explicit about what it
has not decided.

| Harrismith has **not** decided | Status | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Who holds publication / exchange authority | **UNRESOLVED** | BEP §9.7; IM matrix D4 |
| Who holds recipient acceptance authority | **UNRESOLVED** | IM matrix D7 |
| Level of information need | **Not defined** | Model matrix §4 |
| Naming, coordinates, titleblocks, templates standards | **Not established** | BEP §11.12, §13.3 |

Five further items exist (appointing party, lead delivery party,
governance-change authority by class, retention approach, UD-001). **Show four,
say there are more.**

## 5. Simplification and omission

| Simplify | Omit |
|---|---|
| Five rows per column | The full nine-row comparison |
| Four unresolved items | The full unresolved-matters tables from three separate sources |
| One central message, one secondary | Any real organisation's template, named or characterised |
| — | Any suggestion that the comparison comes from the Harrismith documents |

## 6. Labelling requirement

The teaching-synthesis status must be **said**, whether or not it appears on the
slide. If the presenter is asked "where does the BEP say that?", the answer is:
it does not — this is how I am explaining it.

See [`source-map.md`](../../../module-01-what-is-a-bep/source-map.md) subject 3
and section 5.

## 7. Overclaim risk

**Moderate, and specifically about attribution rather than content.** The
comparison is reasonable and useful; the danger is a slide that sits inside a
Harrismith-branded deck and is therefore assumed to be Harrismith's position.
