## 1. Purpose and five-second takeaway

**Purpose.** Teach cell grammar and fix the limit of what any cell asserts

**Five-second takeaway.** *A cell says which function a role holds. Nothing more.*

**Why this form.** Four separate bars prevent the production rows reading as a flowchart. **The `D4` row must be shown as the source records it — all nine cells** — because the contrast is not just *unresolved versus established*: four of `D4`'s cells are **resolved**, and abbreviating them away would teach a row that does not exist.

**Simplification rule.** The census may reduce to `297 cells · none blank · Au ×2 · Ac ×1`. **The `P1`–`P4` bars may not be merged into one arrow**, and **the four `—` cells of `D4` may not be dropped, summarised or replaced by an ellipsis** — the row is shown complete or not at all.

## 7. Connector semantics

**Leader lines only**, from the row and the column into the cell.

**No arrow between `P1`, `P2`, `P3` and `P4`** — an arrow would assert a sequence
the source does not state.

**No connector touches the `D4` block.**

## 8. Status and warning treatment

**Mandatory warning: The two status labels on `P4` and `D4`, **and** a line stating a cell does not record a name, an appointment, occurrence or independence**

`P4` and `D4` each carry their own status label, and the two must be readable
together — the contrast is the teaching point.

**`NO ALLOCATION MADE` is not optional.** Without it, five adjacent `TBD` cells
read as a shortlist of candidate authorities, which BEP §9.7 rules out by name
for the BIM Manager, BIM Coordinator, CDE Administrator, Architect and Lead
Delivery Party.

**Status is carried by words, never by colour alone.** Minimum visible type
**14 pt**; principal labels **≥ 18 pt**; text contrast **≥ 4.5 : 1**; border and
connector contrast **≥ 3 : 1**.

## 11. Accessibility and projection requirements

**Reading order:** intersection → cell meaning → four rows → `D4` role headings →
nine cells → status labels → census.

Combined values are read aloud as *"holds both functions, in no stated order"*.

**Each `D4` cell must be read with its role name**, so the five/four split
survives audio.

**No information is carried by colour alone.** Connector meaning is carried by
**form and label**. Warnings remain legible at projection scale.

## 13. Producer-failure test

**Is there an arrow anywhere between `P1` and `P4`?** If yes, remove it.

**Does the `D4` row show exactly nine cells — five `TBD` and four `—`?** If any
cell is missing, blank, or carries a different value, **STOP**.

**Ask a reader who holds publication authority. If the answer names any of the
five `TBD` roles, `NO ALLOCATION MADE` has failed.**

## 14. STOP conditions

**Stop and refer back to the accepted plan if this visual would:**

- Convert an allocation into evidence of performance
- Claim independence
- Resolve any `TRN-E03` blocker
- **State that `D4` is `TBD` in every column, omit its four `—` cells, or present its five `TBD` cells as an allocation, a shortlist or a set of candidate authorities**
