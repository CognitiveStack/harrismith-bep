## 1. Purpose and five-second takeaway

**Purpose.** Show the origination chain, and defuse the six-codes-six-organisations misreading

**Five-second takeaway.** *Six containers, six codes — four parties.*

**Why this form.** The party boundary must be a **drawn enclosure**, not a colour or a caption — enclosure is what makes "one party, three task teams" unarguable.

**Simplification rule.** Container labels may reduce to their refs. **The MEP enclosure and the `COORD-01` separation may never be dropped.**

## 4. Fixed layout

**Four-stage chain across the top** — party → task team → discipline →
container.

**Beneath it:** six container blocks.

- **MEC, ELE and PLM are enclosed in one visible party boundary** labelled
  *MEP Consultant*.
- **FIR sits outside that enclosure, visibly separate.**

**`COORD-01` sits below and outside the six**, horizontally offset, joined by a
**dashed line of different weight**.

## 6. Geometry or spatial relationships

**Reference canvas: `960 × 540 pt`, 16:9. Origin top left; `x` increases right,
`y` increases down. Title zone `y = 0–72` is reserved. Side margins ≥ 48 pt.**

Chain `y = 96–160`, four boxes across `x = 48–912`, three arrows.

Containers `y = 190–360`. **The MEP enclosure surrounds MEC / ELE / PLM with
≥ 12 pt internal padding.** **FIR sits with ≥ 40 pt clear separation from that
enclosure.**

`COORD-01` `y = 396–460`, **horizontally offset from the six**.

## 7. Connector semantics

**Chain arrows mean "resolves to".**

**The `COORD-01` line means "aggregates from"** and is drawn **dashed** to mark
it as temporary and non-authoring.

**No line implies ownership**, and no line joins `COORD-01` into the row of six.

## 8. Status and warning treatment

**Mandatory warning: `PROPOSED GOVERNANCE` on the container block, and **`Intended governance — not an inventory of what exists in the CDE`****

`PROPOSED GOVERNANCE` sits on the container block itself, not in a corner.

The *intended governance, not an inventory* line is mandatory — without it, six
drawn containers read as six existing models.

**Status is carried by words, never by colour alone.** Minimum visible type
**14 pt**; principal labels **≥ 18 pt**; text contrast **≥ 4.5 : 1**; border and
connector contrast **≥ 3 : 1**.

## 9. Build or reveal sequence

1. Chain.
2. Six containers.
3. **MEP enclosure** — drawn before any emphasis is added elsewhere.
4. FIR separation emphasis.
5. `COORD-01`.

## 10. Mandatory omissions

- No format, no date, no person, no level of information need
- **No implication that six discipline codes mean six organisations**
- No live-inventory tick or "exists" marker
- No function-code grammar — the codes belong to the IM matrix and to Slide 6

## 11. Accessibility and projection requirements

**Reading order:** chain → containers → enclosure → FIR → `COORD-01`.

**The enclosure must be described in words**, since a box outline is invisible to
a screen reader. The dashed `COORD-01` line must be described as *aggregates
from*, not left to line style.

**No information is carried by colour alone.** Connector meaning is carried by
**form and label**. Warnings remain legible at projection scale.

## 12. Screen-reader or presenter-notes description

> *A four-stage chain: party, task team, discipline, information container. Below, six discipline containers. Three of them — mechanical, electrical and plumbing — are enclosed within a single boundary labelled MEP Consultant. Fire sits outside that boundary as a separate party. Four parties in total. Below and offset, COORD-01, a coordination construct joined by a dashed aggregation line; it is not a seventh discipline container. All allocations are proposed governance, and this is intended governance, not an inventory.*

## 13. Producer-failure test

**Ask a reader to count the organisations. If the answer is six, the enclosure
has failed.**

**Ask whether `COORD-01` is a seventh discipline container. If yes, the offset
and line weight have failed.**

## 14. STOP conditions

**Stop and refer back to the accepted plan if this visual would:**

- Apply the IM grammar to the container matrix
- Convert a discipline into an organisation
- Convert an allocation into evidence of performance
- Introduce a date, frequency, format or final deliverable

The full twenty-six module-wide STOP conditions are in
[`README.md`](README.md) §7 and in the accepted plan §3.
