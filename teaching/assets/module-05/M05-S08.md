## 1. Purpose and five-second takeaway

**Purpose.** Show that a functional separation is not a separation of people

**Five-second takeaway.** *Two columns. Possibly one person. Independence not established.*

**Why this form.** The boundary surviving the overlap **is** the teaching point: functions stay distinct even when the person does not.

**Simplification rule.** The participant outline may reduce to a simple rounded rectangle. **The boundary may never be hidden, dashed away or drawn behind the outline.**

**`NOT DEMONSTRATED` is not a failure claim.** No independence evidence exists, and the sources expressly permit role combination. `TA-02` records that exercising a role for training creates **no** professional authority, appointment, duty or liability. **The register records no non-conformance, and none is asserted.**

## 4. Fixed layout

**Two role boxes**, `Author` and `Checker`, with a **solid boundary between them
that remains unbroken**.

**One unnamed participant outline spans both**, drawn with **no fill** so the
boundary **stays visible through the overlap**.

**Beneath:** **`INDEPENDENCE — NOT ESTABLISHED`** in words, and the *not a
defect* line.

## 5. Exact visible wording

**Every string below is mandatory on the slide.**

- `Author` · `Checker`
- `One participant may hold more than one role`
- `A self-check is still a checking act against a defined requirement`
- **`INDEPENDENCE — NOT ESTABLISHED`**
- `TA-02 — no professional authority, appointment, duty or liability`
- `Not a defect · not a non-conformance`

## 6. Geometry or spatial relationships

**Reference canvas: `960 × 540 pt`, 16:9. Origin top left; `x` increases right,
`y` increases down. Title zone `y = 0–72` is reserved. Side margins ≥ 48 pt.**

Role boxes `x = 160–460` and `x = 500–800`, `y = 140–300`.

**Boundary line at `x = 480`, full height of the boxes, drawn on top of
everything else.**

Participant outline `x = 130–830`, `y = 120–320`, **no fill**, so the boundary
reads through it.

Status line `y = 350–390`, centred.

## 7. Connector semantics

**None. The overlap is spatial, not a connector.**

**No arrow between Author and Checker** — the relationship is that they are
distinct, and an arrow would suggest a handover.

Not applicable — no connector is permitted.

## 8. Status and warning treatment

**Mandatory warning: **`INDEPENDENCE — NOT ESTABLISHED`**, and the *not a defect* line — without the second, the slide reads as an accusation**

**Two labels are mandatory and neither may be dropped.**

`INDEPENDENCE — NOT ESTABLISHED` states the limitation. The *not a defect · not a
non-conformance* line prevents the slide reading as an allegation.

**Neither is carried by colour.**

**Status is carried by words, never by colour alone.** Minimum visible type
**14 pt**; principal labels **≥ 18 pt**; text contrast **≥ 4.5 : 1**; border and
connector contrast **≥ 3 : 1**.

## 9. Build or reveal sequence

1. Two role boxes.
2. **Boundary**, drawn on top.
3. Participant outline, no fill.
4. Status line.
5. *Not a defect* line.

## 10. Mandatory omissions

- **No person icon, no silhouette, no headcount, no name**
- No warning triangle, cross or defect symbol
- No Module 6 assurance step, sampling rule or remedy
- No claim that independence exists, anywhere

## 11. Accessibility and projection requirements

**Reading order:** Author → boundary → Checker → participant → status → *not a
defect*.

**The overlap is described in words** — a no-fill outline crossing a line is
meaningless to a screen reader, so the alt text states that one unnamed
participant spans both boxes and that the boundary remains visible.

**No information is carried by colour alone.** Connector meaning is carried by
**form and label**. Warnings remain legible at projection scale.

## 12. Screen-reader or presenter-notes description

> *Two role boxes, Author and Checker, separated by a solid boundary. A single unnamed participant outline spans both boxes; the boundary remains visible through it. Beneath: independence — not established. One participant may hold more than one role; a self-check is still a checking act against a defined requirement; the combination is recorded. This is not a defect and not a non-conformance. Under TA-02, exercising a role for training creates no professional authority, appointment, duty or liability.*

not, redraw.

**Does anything on the slide look like a warning symbol?** If yes, remove it.
