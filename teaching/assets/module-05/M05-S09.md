## 1. Purpose and five-second takeaway

**Purpose.** Show the anatomy of the planning instrument, and separate the four misread fields

**Five-second takeaway.** *Sixteen fields. A plan, not a record.*

**Why this form.** Grouping aids recall; individual visibility protects the controlled names. **Both requirements are absolute and neither may be traded off.**

**Simplification rule.** Group headings may shorten. **No field name may be renamed, merged, abbreviated or omitted — all sixteen appear exactly once.**

**None.** Fields are grouped **by containment**, not connected — a connector
would imply processing order through the sixteen.

Not applicable — no connector is permitted.

## 8. Status and warning treatment

**Mandatory warning: `PROPOSED GOVERNANCE` **and** `A row is a plan, not a record`**

Two statements are mandatory: `PROPOSED GOVERNANCE`, and `A row is a plan, not a
record`.

**The caution markers must have text equivalents**, not shape-only meaning — a
small symbol beside a field name is invisible to a screen reader and ambiguous at
projection scale.

**Status is carried by words, never by colour alone.** Minimum visible type
**14 pt**; principal labels **≥ 18 pt**; text contrast **≥ 4.5 : 1**; border and
connector contrast **≥ 3 : 1**.

## 9. Build or reveal sequence

4. Caution panel.

## 10. Mandatory omissions

- **No example value of any kind**
- No date, no format, no milestone, no deliverable
- No row that looks like a completed transaction
- **No renamed, merged or omitted field name**

## 11. Accessibility and projection requirements

**Reading order:** group 1 → 6, then caption, then cautions.

**The caution markers must have text equivalents.** The grouping caption must be
legible at projection scale — if it is not, enlarge it before shortening
anything else.

**No information is carried by colour alone.** Connector meaning is carried by
**form and label**. Warnings remain legible at projection scale.

## 12. Screen-reader or presenter-notes description

> *Six teaching groups containing sixteen approved schedule fields, each named once: Delivery ID; Exchange slash Milestone; Information Container; Originating Party; Task Team; Discipline; Lead; Recipient; Purpose; Format; State slash Suitability; Checking Requirement; Authorisation Requirement; Acceptance Criteria; Status; Dependencies.
