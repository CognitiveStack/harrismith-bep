## 1. Purpose and five-second takeaway

**Purpose.** Show governance working by stopping, and that the block is five independent matters

**Five-second takeaway.** *Five independent matters. Every one typed. None blank.*

**Why this form.** Five gates make independence **structural** — five separate closures, not one barrier. **The typed status inside each gate is the content, not a label on it.**

**Simplification rule.** Matter names may shorten. **The status text may never be shortened, abbreviated or replaced by a symbol.**

## 6. Geometry or spatial relationships

**Reference canvas: `960 × 540 pt`, 16:9. Origin top left; `x` increases right,
`y` increases down. Title zone `y = 0–72` is reserved. Side margins ≥ 48 pt.**

Five gates across `x = 48–780`, **equal width ≈ 134 pt**, `y = 130–330`, gaps
≈ 12 pt.

**Status text occupies the lower third of each gate.**

Variance note `x = 800–912`, `y = 130–330`, **visibly smaller** so it cannot read
as a sixth gate.

Consequence line `y = 356–396`, full width. Boundary footer `y = 420–480`.

## 7. Connector semantics

**None between the gates** — a connector would imply that clearing one leads to
the next, which is **exactly the misreading this slide exists to prevent**.

Not applicable — no connector is permitted.

## 8. Status and warning treatment

**Mandatory warning: **`T4 BLOCKED — INFORMATION REMAINS SHARED`**, and each gate's own typed status**

**Each gate's status text is its content, not a label on it.** A gate with a
symbol instead of words has failed.

`T4 BLOCKED — INFORMATION REMAINS SHARED` runs beneath all five.

**The closed state is described in words**, never by icon alone.

**Status is carried by words, never by colour alone.** Minimum visible type
**14 pt**; principal labels **≥ 18 pt**; text contrast **≥ 4.5 : 1**; border and
connector contrast **≥ 3 : 1**.

## 10. Mandatory omissions

- **No blank or empty box**
- **No sixth primary gate**
- No gate shown opening or opened
- No progress bar, and no "4 of 5 resolved" device
- No Module 6 procedure — no delivery-review, approval, acceptance or assurance mechanic

## 11. Accessibility and projection requirements

**Reading order:** gate 1 → 5, consequence, variance, boundary.

**The closed state is described in words**, never by icon alone. Each gate's
status text is read with its matter, so the five/three relationship between
matters and terms survives audio.

**No information is carried by colour alone.** Connector meaning is carried by
**form and label**. Warnings remain legible at projection scale.

## 13. Producer-failure test

**Is any gate empty, or does any carry a symbol instead of its status words?** If
yes, **STOP**.

**Does the variance note look like a sixth gate?** If yes, reduce and reposition
it.

**Does anything claim five distinct status terms?** There are **three**.
