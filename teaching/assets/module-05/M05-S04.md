## 1. Purpose and five-second takeaway

**Purpose.** Show what the matrix allocates, and that its allocations do not share one status

**Five-second takeaway.** *Functions down, roles across — and two rows with very different authority.*

**Why this form.** The structural idea is the teaching point. Showing 297 values would invite reading, and the codes are not defined until Slide 6.

**Simplification rule.** Group names may abbreviate. **Role column count may never be reduced below nine**, and **no cell may be populated.**

## 4. Fixed layout

**Matrix skeleton** — seven labelled subject-group bands down the left, **nine**
role columns across the top, **cells showing no values**.

**Two call-outs to the right**, each joined to its row by a short leader line:

| Call-out | Row | Text |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | An **established** row | `ESTABLISHED — BEP §9.4` |
| 2 | The **unresolved** row | `UNRESOLVED — BEP §9.7 · TBD ×5 · — ×4 · no allocation made` |

**Call-out 2 must distinguish the five unresolved cells from the four resolved
no-function cells.**

## 7. Connector semantics

**Leader lines only** — they point at a row and mean nothing else.

**No arrows between the grid and the call-outs**, and no connector anywhere
inside the grid.

## 8. Status and warning treatment

**Mandatory warning: **Both call-outs, together.** One alone flattens the split rule**

**Both call-outs are mandatory and must appear together.** Call-out 1 alone would
suggest the matrix is settled; call-out 2 alone would suggest it is empty.

**Call-out 2 carries two counts, not one.** `TBD` ×5 and `—` ×4 are separate
facts and must be readable as separate facts.

**Status is carried by words, never by colour alone.** Minimum visible type
**14 pt**; principal labels **≥ 18 pt**; text contrast **≥ 4.5 : 1**; border and
connector contrast **≥ 3 : 1**.

## 9. Build or reveal sequence

1. Grid frame.
2. Row bands.
3. Column headings.
4. Established call-out.
5. Unresolved call-out — **with both counts and `no allocation made`**.

## 10. Mandatory omissions

- **No function code anywhere** — the codes are defined on Slide 6
- No person, no company
- No appointment chart or organisation chart
- No tick, cross or completion mark
- **No populated cell**

## 11. Accessibility and projection requirements

**Reading order:** title → grid dimensions → group names → role names → call-out
1 → call-out 2.

**Grid emptiness is stated in words** in the alt text — an empty table is
ambiguous to a screen reader. **The `TBD` / `—` split in call-out 2 must be read
as two separate counts**, never as one.

**No information is carried by colour alone.** Connector meaning is carried by
**form and label**. Warnings remain legible at projection scale.

## 12. Screen-reader or presenter-notes description

> *A matrix skeleton: seven subject-group bands down the left, nine functional-role columns across the top, thirty-three function rows in total. The cells are shown empty for teaching; the source matrix is fully populated. Two call-outs: one marking a row the BEP expressly establishes — Task-Team Lead authorises sharing, BEP 9.4; one marking a row the BEP expressly establishes as unresolved — publication authority, BEP 9.7. That row holds nine populated cells: five TBD, against Appointing Party, Lead Delivery Party, BIM Manager, BIM Coordinator and Task-Team Lead; and four dashes, against Author, Checker, CDE Administration and Recipient. No allocation is made.*

## 13. Producer-failure test

**Does any cell contain a character?** If yes, the visual has pre-empted Slides 6
and 7.

**Do both call-outs appear?** If only one, the split rule is broken.

**Does the unresolved call-out say `TBD` in every column?** If yes, **STOP** —
four of those nine cells are `—`.

## 14. STOP conditions

**Stop and refer back to the accepted plan if this visual would:**

- Apply the IM grammar to the container matrix or the schedule
- Flatten every IM allocation to **proposed**
- Flatten every IM allocation to **established**
- Convert a role into a person
- Convert an allocation into evidence of performance
- **State that `D4` is `TBD` in every column, omit its four `—` cells, or present its five `TBD` cells as an allocation, a shortlist or a set of candidate authorities**

The full twenty-six module-wide STOP conditions are in
[`README.md`](README.md) §7 and in the accepted plan §3.
