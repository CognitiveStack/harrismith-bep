## 1. Purpose and five-second takeaway

**Purpose.** Transfer the method, and answer nothing on Triviron's behalf

**Five-second takeaway.** *Every field says: not yet established.*

**Why this form.** **A visible, permanently empty answer area per group is what makes the slide structurally incapable of asserting a Triviron answer.**

**Simplification rule.** Questions may reduce to keywords. **No answer area may be removed, and none may be filled — including with an example.**

## 7. Connector semantics

**None. No arrow from a Harrismith example to a Triviron question** — that would
be a recommendation drawn as a line.

Not applicable — no connector is permitted.

## 8. Status and warning treatment

**Mandatory warning: `NOT YET ESTABLISHED` ×5, **and** the closing marker**

**`NOT YET ESTABLISHED` appears five times, once per answer area, and is the only
text in those areas.**

The Harrismith panel carries **three different statuses**, not one. If it shows a
single status for all three resources, **the split-status requirement has
failed**.

The closing marker is the last element on the slide.

**Status is carried by words, never by colour alone.** Minimum visible type
**14 pt**; principal labels **≥ 18 pt**; text contrast **≥ 4.5 : 1**; border and
connector contrast **≥ 3 : 1**.

## 11. Accessibility and projection requirements

**Reading order:** group 1 → 5, **each answer area read as `not yet
established`**, then the Harrismith panel, then the closing marker.

**The subordination of the Harrismith panel must be stated in words**, since
styling alone does not survive audio.

**No information is carried by colour alone.** Connector meaning is carried by
**form and label**. Warnings remain legible at projection scale.

## 13. Producer-failure test

**Cover the closing strip. Can a reader extract a single Triviron answer from
anything on the slide?** If yes, **STOP**.

**Does the Harrismith panel state one status for all three resources?** If yes,
the split-status requirement has failed.

## 14. STOP conditions

**Stop and refer back to the accepted plan if this visual would:**

- Flatten every IM allocation to **proposed**
- Flatten every IM allocation to **established**
- Introduce a date, frequency, format or final deliverable
- **Invent a Triviron fact or answer**
- Suggest the content baseline is already a produced presentation
