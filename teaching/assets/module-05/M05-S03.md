## 1. Purpose and five-second takeaway

**Purpose.** Give a durable way to tell the three resources apart, and put the RACI refusal on the record

**Five-second takeaway.** *Three questions. RACI was refused, and the BEP says why.*

**Why this form.** Equal panels prevent hierarchy. A quotation block makes the refusal governance rather than opinion. A one-way chain shows dependency without implying process order.

**Simplification rule.** The *does not answer* lines may compress to two words each. **The refusal quotation and its reference may not be shortened.**

## 4. Fixed layout

**Three equal panels**, one question each, each carrying its own *does not
answer* line.

**Beneath them:** the RACI refusal as a **quotation block with its BEP
reference**.

**Beneath that:** a **one-way dependency chain** — schedule → container matrix →
function matrix, drawn with **two arrows, both pointing the same way**.

## 5. Exact visible wording

**Every string below is mandatory on the slide.**

- The three questions, **verbatim** from the resources
- Each panel's *does not answer* line
- **`RACI is not adopted`** with **`BEP §5.12`**
- The recorded reason — *collapses checking from authorising, coordinating from performing*
- *"No container is scheduled that does not exist in that matrix"*

## 6. Geometry or spatial relationships

**Reference canvas: `960 × 540 pt`, 16:9. Origin top left; `x` increases right,
`y` increases down. Title zone `y = 0–72` is reserved. Side margins ≥ 48 pt.**

Panels `y = 96–260`, **equal thirds** across `x = 48–912`.

Refusal block `y = 284–372`, centred, width ≈ 600 pt.

Chain `y = 396–460`, three labelled boxes with **two arrows, both pointing
left**.

## 7. Connector semantics

**Arrows mean "depends on / consumes from", one direction only.**

**No arrow returns.** **No arrow between the three upper panels** — they are
peers, and a connector would create a hierarchy the sources do not state.

Each arrow carries the visible label *depends on*.

## 8. Status and warning treatment

**Mandatory warning: The **`BEP §5.12`** reference beside the refusal — without it the refusal reads as preference**

The refusal is set as a **quotation with its source reference visible**. Without
the reference it reads as the author's preference rather than as governance.

The chain sits **below** the refusal, so the reading order is *three questions →
why not RACI → how they connect*.

**Status is carried by words, never by colour alone.** Minimum visible type
**14 pt**; principal labels **≥ 18 pt**; text contrast **≥ 4.5 : 1**; border and
connector contrast **≥ 3 : 1**.

## 9. Build or reveal sequence

1. Three panels.
2. Refusal block.
3. Chain.

## 10. Mandatory omissions

- No R/A/C/I mapping, in either direction
- No combined or merged grid
- No ranking or numbering that implies seniority between the resources
- No bidirectional arrow, and no arrow returning to the schedule

## 11. Accessibility and projection requirements

**Reading order:** panels left → right, then refusal, then chain.

**Arrow direction is stated in the alt text**, not inferred from shape. The
quotation's source reference is read aloud with the quotation.

**No information is carried by colour alone.** Connector meaning is carried by
**form and label**. Warnings remain legible at projection scale.

## 12. Screen-reader or presenter-notes description

> *Three equal panels, one question each, each stating what it does not answer. Below, a quotation: RACI is not adopted, BEP section 5.12, because it collapses checking from authorising and coordinating from performing. Below that, a one-way chain: the delivery schedule depends on the container matrix, which depends on the function matrix. Nothing depends on the schedule.*

## 13. Producer-failure test

**Can a reader point at which resource depends on which, and state that nothing
depends on the schedule?** If the arrows are ambiguous, redraw.

**Second test.** Do the letters R, A, C or I appear anywhere on the slide? If
yes, **STOP**.

## 14. STOP conditions

**Stop and refer back to the accepted plan if this visual would:**

- Merge the three resources
- Present RACI as the grammar, or map any code to R/A/C/I
- Imply both matrices share one coded vocabulary

The full twenty-six module-wide STOP conditions are in
[`README.md`](README.md) §7 and in the accepted plan §3.

source, because there is no diagram source to render.**
