## 1. Purpose and five-second takeaway

**Purpose.** Establish the central question from BEP §10.5, and separate what is allocated from what has been observed

**Five-second takeaway.** *Six allocated. One observed. And that is not an accusation.*

**Why this form.** The caution must be structurally inseparable from the observation. Placing it in a footnote or a separate box invites the two halves to be read apart.

**Simplification rule.** The eight fields may compress to single words. **The caution may never be shortened, moved outside the observed frame, or reduced below 14 pt.**

## 7. Connector semantics

**None between the columns.** An arrow would imply that allocation *leads to*
observation — a pipeline with five failures in it.

Not applicable — no connector is permitted.

## 8. Status and warning treatment

**Mandatory warning: The absence-of-observation sentence, **inside the observed column's frame****

The caution is **part of the observed column**, not an annotation on it.

The five unobserved containers must be typographically **identical** to the
observed one apart from their status word. **No red, no warning icon, no
emphasis that reads as censure.**

**Status is carried by words, never by colour alone.** Minimum visible type
**14 pt**; principal labels **≥ 18 pt**; text contrast **≥ 4.5 : 1**; border and
connector contrast **≥ 3 : 1**.

## 9. Build or reveal sequence

1. Eight fields.
2. Strike-through and replacement.
3. Allocated column.
4. Observed column — **with its caution already present**, never added afterwards.

## 10. Mandatory omissions

- No date, no calendar, no frequency, no timeline
- No red or warning iconography on the five unobserved containers
- No "missing", "outstanding" or "overdue" label
- No suggestion that any discipline is behind, absent or uncommitted

## 11. Accessibility and projection requirements

**Reading order:** eight fields → allocated → observed → caution.

**The strike-through must be accompanied by the replacement text** — a line
through *by when* is invisible to a screen reader, so *for which event* must be
present as text, not implied.

**No information is carried by colour alone.** Connector meaning is carried by
**form and label**. Warnings remain legible at projection scale.

## 12. Screen-reader or presenter-notes description

> *Top: the eight things each task team should be able to answer, from BEP section 10.5. The phrase 'by when' is struck through and replaced by 'for which event'. Below, two columns. Allocated: six containers. Observed: ARC-01 only, at the inspected level, status observed-qualified; the other five, not demonstrated. Inside the observed column: absence of observation is not observation of absence.*

## 13. Producer-failure test

**Show the lower band alone to someone who has not heard the talk.** If they
conclude five disciplines are behind, **the visual has failed**.

**Second test.** Is the caution inside the observed column's border? If it has
drifted outside, redraw.

## 14. STOP conditions

**Stop and refer back to the accepted plan if this visual would:**

- Convert an allocation into evidence of performance
- Treat a schedule row as a live exchange
- Introduce a date, frequency, format or final deliverable
- Suggest the content baseline is already a produced presentation
