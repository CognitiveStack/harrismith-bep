## 1. Purpose and five-second takeaway

**Purpose.** Introduce the three events separately, and teach that their statuses mean three different things

**Five-second takeaway.** *Three events, and no two are in the same condition.*

**Why this form.** Identical size prevents a status reading as importance. Side-by-side placement **without connectors** prevents the three reading as stages.

**Simplification rule.** Card body text may compress. **The three status labels are quoted controlled wording and may not be shortened or paraphrased.**

## 4. Fixed layout

**Three cards of identical size**, side by side, **each with its exact status
label in a band across its top**.

| Card | Status band |
|---|---|
| `TRN-E01` | `PROPOSED` |
| `TRN-E02` | `PROPOSED · CONDITIONAL` |
| `TRN-E03` | `PROPOSED — BLOCKED PENDING GOVERNANCE DECISIONS` |

`TRN-E02`'s card carries a **per-affected-container** marker. `TRN-E03`'s carries
a **forward pointer to Slide 12**.

**The cards never touch, and nothing joins them.**

## 6. Geometry or spatial relationships

**Reference canvas: `960 × 540 pt`, 16:9. Origin top left; `x` increases right,
`y` increases down. Title zone `y = 0–72` is reserved. Side margins ≥ 48 pt.**

Three cards, **equal width ≈ 272 pt**, `x = 48–912`, `y = 110–420`, gaps ≈ 24 pt.

**Status band = the top 48 pt of each card.**

Caution strip `y = 440–480`, full width.

**Cards never touch.**

## 7. Connector semantics

**NONE PERMITTED.** No arrow between the cards, in any direction.

A sequence arrow would assert a mandatory order the sources do not state — the
three are **concepts, not stages**.

Not applicable — no connector is permitted.

## 8. Status and warning treatment

**Mandatory warning: All three status labels, **and** the *blocked is not overdue* line**

**Status is carried by the words in each band, never by band colour.**

The *blocked is not overdue, rejected or unfinished* line is mandatory. Without
it, the third card reads as a project failure rather than as governance working
by stopping.

**Status is carried by words, never by colour alone.** Minimum visible type
**14 pt**; principal labels **≥ 18 pt**; text contrast **≥ 4.5 : 1**; border and
connector contrast **≥ 3 : 1**.

## 9. Build or reveal sequence

1. Three cards **together** — never one at a time, which would imply sequence.
2. Status bands.
3. Per-container marker on `TRN-E02`.
4. Caution strip.

## 10. Mandatory omissions

- **No timeline, no calendar, no date, no frequency**
- **No arrow, chevron or connector between the cards**
- No numbering that implies order
- No tick, cross or overdue marker
- **No blocking matters** — Slide 12 owns them

## 11. Accessibility and projection requirements

**Reading order:** card 1 → 2 → 3 → caution strip.

**Status is carried by the words in each band, never by band colour.** The
absence of connectors must be stated in the alt text, since "no arrows" is not
otherwise conveyable.

**No information is carried by colour alone.** Connector meaning is carried by
**form and label**. Warnings remain legible at projection scale.

## 12. Screen-reader or presenter-notes description

> *Three cards of equal size, no arrows between them. Card one: TRN-E01, design coordination share, status proposed, event-triggered or TBD, state Shared, coordination use only, six separate exchanges. Card two: TRN-E02, coordination reshare, status proposed and conditional, repeatable with no calendar frequency, activated per affected container only; an unactivated row is not a pending exchange. Card three: TRN-E03, controlled design review, status proposed — blocked pending governance decisions, timing TBD. Below: blocked is not overdue, rejected or unfinished.*

## 13. Producer-failure test

**Is there any arrow, chevron or numeral suggesting order?** If yes, remove it.

**Does `TRN-E03` look late rather than blocked?** If yes, the wording has
failed.
