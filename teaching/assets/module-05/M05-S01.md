## 1. Purpose and five-second takeaway

**Purpose.** Fix the identity and the **status** of all three resources before any content is shown

**Five-second takeaway.** *Three separate documents, and they do not share one status.*

**Why this form.** Three equal panels make separateness structural rather than asserted. A shared footer shows what *is* common without merging what is not.

**Simplification rule.** If space is short, shorten the question text. **Never merge two panels, and never drop a status band.**

## 2. Source and authority basis

**Principal sources.** `S1` §5.12, §7.12, §10.13, §13.6; `S2`/`S3`/`S4` status blocks; `S8` `AD-001`

**Statements implemented.** `M5-S1-01` – `M5-S1-12` — classified in
[`../../module-05-responsibility-matrices-and-information-delivery-planning/source-map.md`](../../module-05-responsibility-matrices-and-information-delivery-planning/source-map.md) §5.

**Controlling specification.** ``M5V-01`` in
[`../../module-05-responsibility-matrices-and-information-delivery-planning/visual-demonstration-plan.md`](../../module-05-responsibility-matrices-and-information-delivery-planning/visual-demonstration-plan.md).

**Authority chain.**

```text
controlled Harrismith source
  ↓
accepted Module 5 teaching content
  ↓
accepted visual-demonstration plan
  ↓
Module 5 slide-source file          ← this file
  ↓
future presentation assembly package
```

**The order runs downward and never upward.** A later render or PowerPoint
**never becomes authoritative**. Where this file and the accepted plan differ,
**the plan wins**; where the plan and the accepted teaching content differ, **the
teaching content wins**; where any teaching material conflicts with a controlled
Harrismith source, **the controlled source wins**.

## 4. Fixed layout

**Three vertical panels of equal width and height**, side by side, each carrying
**its own status band beneath it**. **One shared footer strip** runs the full
width and carries the approval and publication position.

| Panel | Resource | Its own status band |
|---|---|---|
| Left | Information Management Responsibility Matrix | **Split rule** |
| Centre | Model / Information Responsibility Matrix | `PROPOSED GOVERNANCE` |
| Right | Information Delivery Schedule | `PROPOSED GOVERNANCE` |

**The panels never touch, and they are never merged into one table.**

## 5. Exact visible wording

**Every string below is mandatory on the slide.**

- The three resources' questions, one per panel
- `APPROVED WITH CONDITIONS — Training Baseline 0.1`
- `AD-001`
- `PROPOSED GOVERNANCE` — on the `S3` and `S4` panels
- The **split rule** — on the `S2` panel
- **`PUBLICATION NOT AUTHORISED`** — in the shared footer

## 6. Geometry or spatial relationships

**Reference canvas: `960 × 540 pt`, 16:9. Origin top left; `x` increases right,
`y` increases down. Title zone `y = 0–72` is reserved. Side margins ≥ 48 pt.**

Three panels across `x = 48–912`, **equal width ≈ 272 pt**, gaps ≈ 24 pt,
`y = 96–400`. **Status band = the lower 64 pt of each panel.** Footer strip
`y = 424–492`, full width.

**Panels never touch.** The gaps are load-bearing — they are what makes the three
resources read as separate documents rather than three columns of one table.

## 7. Connector semantics

**None. No connector of any kind is permitted on this slide.**

No arrows and no lines between the panels — a connection would imply a
relationship this slide does not teach. The dependency chain belongs to Slide 3.

## 8. Status and warning treatment

**Mandatory warning: `PUBLICATION NOT AUTHORISED` in the shared footer, and **one status band per panel****

Each panel carries **its own** status band; the footer carries **only** what is
genuinely shared — the approval decision and the publication hold.

**Cover the footer and the three content classifications must still be
readable.** That is the whole design.

**Status is carried by words, never by colour alone.** Minimum visible type
**14 pt**; principal labels **≥ 18 pt**; text contrast **≥ 4.5 : 1**; border and
connector contrast **≥ 3 : 1**.

## 9. Build or reveal sequence

1. Three panels together — never one at a time, which would imply ranking.
2. Status bands.
3. Footer strip.

## 10. Mandatory omissions

- No combined or simplified single-table view of the three resources
- No person, no company, no role holder
- No implementation tick, badge or "live" indicator
- No claim that a deck has been produced

## 11. Accessibility and projection requirements

**Reading order:** left panel → centre panel → right panel → footer.

Status words are **never colour-only**. All status text **≥ 18 pt**. The panel
separation must be described in words in the alt text, since gaps are invisible
to a screen reader.

**No information is carried by colour alone.** Connector meaning is carried by
**form and label**. Warnings remain legible at projection scale.

## 12. Screen-reader or presenter-notes description

> *Three separate panels. Panel one: Information Management Responsibility Matrix — who performs which information-management function; status, split rule. Panel two: Model / Information Responsibility Matrix — who produces and maintains which information container; status, proposed governance. Panel three: Information Delivery Schedule — what is exchanged, at what event, to whom, why, in what form; status, proposed governance. Footer: all three approved with conditions under AD-001; publication not authorised.*

## 13. Producer-failure test

**Cover the footer.** Can a reader still tell that `S3` and `S4` are
`PROPOSED GOVERNANCE` and that `S2` is split? **If not, the status bands have
failed.**

**Second test.** Does any element visually join two panels? If yes, remove it.

## 14. STOP conditions

**Stop and refer back to the accepted plan if this visual would:**

- Merge the three resources, or present any combined table
- Flatten every IM allocation to **proposed**
- Flatten every IM allocation to **established**
- Convert a role into a person
- Suggest the content baseline is already a produced presentation

The full twenty-six module-wide STOP conditions are in
[`README.md`](README.md) §7 and in the accepted plan §3.

## 15. Native PowerPoint reconstruction notes

Three rectangles + three text boxes + three status rectangles + one footer rectangle. **No connectors.**

**Native objects only.** No SmartArt, no imported image, no Mermaid, no
Graphviz, no PlantUML. **Nothing on this slide is rendered from a diagram
source, because there is no diagram source to render.**
