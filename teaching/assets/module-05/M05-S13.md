## 1. Purpose and five-second takeaway

**Purpose.** Teach the seven markers as **seven different statements**, two of which are resolved

**Five-second takeaway.** *Seven typed markers. Not one blank. Not all "not yet".*

**Why this form.** **The outcome column is the correction that makes `—` and `Not applicable` visibly resolved.** Without it the panel reads as seven flavours of "unknown".

**Simplification rule.** Examples may shorten to a single ref. **The outcome column may never be dropped, merged into the question column, or reduced to a symbol.**

## 8. Status and warning treatment

**Mandatory warning: The panel label **`Teaching grouping — not a controlled code set`****

**The outcome column carries the status, and it is mandatory.**

Two of the seven outcomes are **RESOLVED**. If the outcome column is dropped or
merged, the panel asserts that all seven are unknown — which is the error this
slide exists to prevent.

**The label strip is equally mandatory.**

**Status is carried by words, never by colour alone.** Minimum visible type
**14 pt**; principal labels **≥ 18 pt**; text contrast **≥ 4.5 : 1**; border and
connector contrast **≥ 3 : 1**.

## 11. Accessibility and projection requirements

**Reading order:** row by row, all four columns.

**The outcome is read aloud with every marker** — the distinction between
*unresolved* and *resolved* must survive audio, since a rule between two rows is
invisible to a screen reader.

**No information is carried by colour alone.** Connector meaning is carried by
**form and label**. Warnings remain legible at projection scale.

## 12. Screen-reader or presenter-notes description

Row two, separated by a rule: dash, does this role act here, resolved — no function, one hundred and thirty-one cells of the IM matrix. Row three: not defined, what content is required, unresolved, final deliverable set. Row four: not established, whose identity or authority, unresolved, recipient identity. Row five: not applicable, does this field apply, resolved — does not apply, COORD-01 originating party. Row six: conditional, does this row activate, trigger-dependent, TRN-E02. Row seven: blocked, can this proceed, cannot proceed, TRN-E03 and T4. Only the IM Responsibility Matrix publishes a legend. Nothing is blank.*

## 13. Producer-failure test

**Ask a reader what `—` means. If the answer contains "unknown", "not yet" or
"blank", the outcome column has failed.**

**Ask whether this is a project code set. If yes, the label strip has failed.**
