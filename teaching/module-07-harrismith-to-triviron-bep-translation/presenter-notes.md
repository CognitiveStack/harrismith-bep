## Slide 1 — Where we are: Harrismith → Triviron — `1.0 min`

- **Opening cue:** "Six modules ago, none of us could read a BEP. Now we've
  worked through a whole management system. Today the question changes."
- **Explanation sequence:** (1) point through the roadmap — Modules 1–6 built
  the foundation on the Harrismith worked example; (2) Module 7 is current —
  Harrismith → Triviron translation; (3) Module 8 comes later — running the
  workshop conversations. (4) Name the deliverable precisely: a **translation
  framework** supporting a future project-specific Triviron BEP.
- **Key sentence:** *"We are moving from understanding Harrismith's system to
  learning how to ask the questions a future Triviron BEP will need."*
- **Boundary warning:** never say "we are creating the Triviron BEP." The
  framework is not the BEP; no Triviron project appointment may be claimed
  (`M7-S10`, `M7-S11`).
- **Example / analogy:** none required — the roadmap itself is the visual.
- **Transition cue:** "Before we translate anything, we fix the rule that
  governs the whole module."

## Slide 2 — Translate the method, not the answers — `1.3 min`

- **Opening cue:** "Everything in this module obeys one boundary. Here it is —
  two columns, no arrow between them."
- **Explanation sequence:** (1) left column — what may transfer as method:
  questions, distinctions, analytical patterns, evidence discipline, typed
  absence, separated functions and acts; (2) right column — what does not
  transfer as an answer: role allocations, responsibility assignments,
  platform arrangements, CDE configuration, checks and `CI-` identifiers,
  tolerances, Issue taxonomy and statuses, authority allocations,
  publication/acceptance arrangements, container names, project triggers;
  (3) stress the equal weight of the two columns — the right column is not a
  caveat.
- **Key sentence:** *"A reference implementation gives us better questions. It
  does not give us another project's answers."* — teaching synthesis of the
  accepted boundary, and say so if asked: the controlled formulation is
  *Harrismith supplies reference patterns, questions and governance
  distinctions; it does not supply Triviron's populated answers.*
- **Boundary warning:** the right column must never be softened into a
  "starter set" or "sensible default" (`M7-S01`–`M7-S09`, `M7-S15`,
  `M7-S16`). Excluding a value from transfer says nothing about what Triviron
  actually does — that would need evidence.
- **Example / analogy (source-safe):** Harrismith's twelve interface checks
  exist as a *pattern* — "a check set is a governed choice" transfers; the
  twelve checks and their `CI-` identifiers do not.
- **Transition cue:** "So what does the work look like, if not copying? It
  looks like a chain — with a hard stop in the middle."

## Slide 3 — The translation chain — `1.5 min`

- **Opening cue:** "This is the whole method on one slide. Nine steps — and
  the most important thing on it is the stop."
- **Explanation sequence:** (1) walk the chain: reference pattern →
  translation question → Triviron evidence required → evidence available /
  missing; (2) halt at the **STOP RULE**, before candidate decision, and give
  its reason: *a Harrismith populated value is evidence about Harrismith — it
  is not evidence that Triviron should adopt the same value*; (3) complete the
  chain — candidate decision, authority required, recorded status, destination
  class; (4) show the current register outcome: 21 rows, evidence available
  `NONE IDENTIFIED` in all 21, zero candidates, zero `PROPOSAL`, zero
  `ESTABLISHED` — the chain currently stops at the boundary, by rule and by
  evidence.
- **Key sentences:** *"No Triviron candidate decision may be derived solely
  from a populated Harrismith value."* · *"Every step may legitimately end in
  typed absence."*
- **Boundary warning:** the arrows are analytical progression, not a pipeline
  — nothing flows along them; and the zeros are the programme's controlled
  evidence position, never proof that no real-world Triviron arrangement
  exists (`M7-S12`, `M7-S15`, `M7-S18`). `NONE IDENTIFIED` is not
  `NONE EXISTS`.
- **Example / analogy:** none required — the register outcome beside the chain
  is the worked demonstration.
- **Transition cue:** "Run that chain twenty-one times and you get Module 7's
  working record — the decision backlog on the next slide."
