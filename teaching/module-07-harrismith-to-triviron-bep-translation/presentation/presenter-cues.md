## 2. Opening — approximately 20 seconds

*"Six modules built a worked example. This one asks what that example is
actually good for — and the honest answer is: better questions, not another
project's answers."*

## 3. Normal closing — approximately 20 seconds

*"Twenty-one questions, every gap typed, every decision waiting on its evidence
and its authority. That is not an unfinished document. That is what a decision
workshop needs before it can start."*

## 4. Emergency closing — ten seconds

*"The method transfers. The answers do not. Module 8 is where we learn to run
the conversations that earn them."*

## 5. The universal fallback

**When a learner confuses method with answer — on any slide — the safe bounded
answer is:**

> *"That is Harrismith's answer, and it stays Harrismith's. What transfers is
> the question it forces us to ask. For Triviron, this programme has not
> identified the evidence that would let anyone answer it — and not having
> identified it is not the same as it not existing."*

**Use it verbatim. It is bounded in both directions.**

## 7. Per-slide cues

### Slide 1 — Where we are: Harrismith → Triviron · 1.0 min

- **Opening cue.** *"Where we are, and — more importantly — what kind of thing
  this module produces."*
- **Central distinction.** Reference implementation ≠ target governance ·
  translation framework ≠ the future BEP · a teaching module ≠ a project
  appointment.
- **Visual-reading cue.** Three equal segments, left to right. The arrows say
  *next module*, nothing more.
- **Boundary cue.** Read the strip in full: **not a project phase, appointment or
  schedule** · **no Triviron BEP exists in this programme**.
- **Fallback.** *"Module 8 is a teaching module, not a scheduled event."*
- **Transition.** *"Before we translate anything, we fix the rule that governs
  every minute of this module."*

### Slide 2 — Translate the method, not the answers · 1.3 min

- **Opening cue.** *"Two lists. Notice what is between them."*
- **Central distinction.** Method ≠ answer · pattern ≠ value · exclusion from
  transfer ≠ a claim about what Triviron does.
- **Visual-reading cue.** Point at the **empty channel** and name it. *"There is
  no bridge, and that is deliberate."*
- **Boundary cue.** The right column is **not** a starter set, not defaults, not
  sensible starting points.
- **Fallback.** The universal fallback (§5).
- **Transition.** *"If answers don't transfer, what does the work look like? A
  chain — with a hard stop in the middle."*

### Slide 3 — The translation chain · 1.5 min

- **Opening cue.** *"Nine objects. One of them stops the other eight."*
- **Central distinction.** Analytical progression ≠ content pipeline. **Nothing
  moves along these arrows.**
- **Visual-reading cue.** Walk the top four, then **stop at the band and stay
  there**. The lower four are *not reached* — not missing, not failed.
- **Boundary cue.** `NONE IDENTIFIED × 21`, candidate decisions `0` — *"the
  chain stops here by rule and by evidence"*.
- **Fallback.** *"Reaching the STOP is the controlled state of the work, not a
  failure of it."*
- **Transition.** *"That chain, run twenty-one times, is our working record."*

### Slide 4 — The decision backlog: 21 questions, zero Triviron answers · 1.3 min

- **Opening cue.** *"Seven domains, three questions each. Twenty-one problems,
  no answers."*
- **Central distinction.** A backlog ≠ a partially completed BEP. The five

- **Visual-reading cue.** Point at the strip label — *enumeration order, not
  progression* — before reading any count.
- **Boundary cue.** The three zeros mean **prerequisite evidence not

- **`T7-L-R2` — say aloud.** **`typed absence is a valid result — zero candidate
  decisions is controlled progress, not failure`.** This left the panel to make
  the panel fit; **it must not leave the room.**
- **Fallback.** *"A completed version of this grid would still hold questions."*
- **Transition.** *"Twenty-one is abstract. Let's walk the seven domains."*

- **Central distinction.** Reference does not constitute approval · a cycle

- **Boundary cue.** Harrismith's purpose text, its document split, its cycle

- **`T7-L-R2` — say aloud.** The Harrismith cycle-wording variance is **the

- **Transition.** *"A governed cycle needs people with defined functions."*

### Logical Slide 6 — Domain 2: Roles and authority · 1.5 min · physical 6–7

- **Central distinction.** Function ≠ person · allocation ≠ holder · allocation ≠

- **Visual-reading cue.** Read **function**, then **holder**, on every card.
  *"Notice there is no chart here, and there will not be one."*

  It is not evidence we have."*
- **`T7-L-R2` — say aloud.** `allocation ≠ holder` · `allocation ≠ performance` ·

- **Fallback.** *"I cannot name anyone, and neither can this programme. That is
  the honest position, not an omission."*
- **Transition, after `PART 2 OF 2`.** *"Functions act on inputs."*
