## 1. Standing delivery rules

| Rule | Requirement |
|---|---|
| **C1** | **Never answer a `TDR-` question for Triviron.** Not as an example, not "just to illustrate", not "off the record" |
| **C2** | **Never deliver a boundary strip as small print.** Read it; it is content |
| **C3** | **Never say a zero is a problem.** Zero candidate decisions is the controlled position |
| **C4** | **Never merge classification and status.** They are two independent fields with one shared word |
| **C5** | **Never say Triviron lacks something.** The programme has not identified it — that is all anyone here knows |
| **C6** | Timing is **allocated, not measured**. Do not claim the deck has been rehearsed or timed |
| **C7** | Advance every slide **manually** |

## 2. Opening — approximately 20 seconds

*"Six modules built a worked example. This one asks what that example is
actually good for — and the honest answer is: better questions, not another
project's answers."*

## 3. Normal closing — approximately 20 seconds

*"Twenty-one questions, every gap typed, every decision waiting on its evidence
and its authority. That is not an unfinished document. That is what a decision
workshop needs before it can start."*

## 4. Emergency closing — ten seconds

*"The method transfers. The answers do not. Module 8 is where we learn to run
the conversations that earn them."*

## 5. The universal fallback

**When a learner confuses method with answer — on any slide — the safe bounded
answer is:**

> *"That is Harrismith's answer, and it stays Harrismith's. What transfers is
> the question it forces us to ask. For Triviron, this programme has not
> identified the evidence that would let anyone answer it — and not having
> identified it is not the same as it not existing."*

**Use it verbatim. It is bounded in both directions.**

## 6. Section recovery

| If time is short | Do this |
|---|---|
| Behind by Slide 4 | Compress Slides 5–11 to their questions and the shared boundary line. **Never drop Slide 3 or Slide 13** |
| Behind by Slide 11 | Merge Slides 12 and 13 into one pass on *classification ≠ status* and *four kinds of unknown* |
| Badly behind | Go to Slide 14 and use the emergency closing. **Slide 14's header declaration is always read in full** |

## 7. Per-slide cues

### Slide 1 — Where we are: Harrismith → Triviron · 1.0 min

- **Opening cue.** *"Where we are, and — more importantly — what kind of thing
  this module produces."*
- **Central distinction.** Reference implementation ≠ target governance ·
  translation framework ≠ the future BEP · a teaching module ≠ a project
  appointment.
- **Visual-reading cue.** Three equal segments, left to right. The arrows say
  *next module*, nothing more.
- **Boundary cue.** Read the strip in full: **not a project phase, appointment or
  schedule** · **no Triviron BEP exists in this programme**.
- **Fallback.** *"Module 8 is a teaching module, not a scheduled event."*
- **Transition.** *"Before we translate anything, we fix the rule that governs
  every minute of this module."*

### Slide 2 — Translate the method, not the answers · 1.3 min

- **Opening cue.** *"Two lists. Notice what is between them."*
- **Central distinction.** Method ≠ answer · pattern ≠ value · exclusion from
  transfer ≠ a claim about what Triviron does.
- **Visual-reading cue.** Point at the **empty channel** and name it. *"There is
  no bridge, and that is deliberate."*
- **Boundary cue.** The right column is **not** a starter set, not defaults, not
  sensible starting points.
- **Fallback.** The universal fallback (§5).
- **Transition.** *"If answers don't transfer, what does the work look like? A
  chain — with a hard stop in the middle."*

### Slide 3 — The translation chain · 1.5 min

- **Opening cue.** *"Nine objects. One of them stops the other eight."*
- **Central distinction.** Analytical progression ≠ content pipeline. **Nothing
  moves along these arrows.**
- **Visual-reading cue.** Walk the top four, then **stop at the band and stay
  there**. The lower four are *not reached* — not missing, not failed.
- **Boundary cue.** `NONE IDENTIFIED × 21`, candidate decisions `0` — *"the
  chain stops here by rule and by evidence"*.
- **Fallback.** *"Reaching the STOP is the controlled state of the work, not a
  failure of it."*
- **Transition.** *"That chain, run twenty-one times, is our working record."*

### Slide 4 — The decision backlog: 21 questions, zero Triviron answers · 1.3 min

- **Opening cue.** *"Seven domains, three questions each. Twenty-one problems,
  no answers."*
- **Central distinction.** A backlog ≠ a partially completed BEP. The five
  classifications are **an enumeration, not a ladder**.
- **Visual-reading cue.** Point at the strip label — *enumeration order, not
  progression* — before reading any count.
- **Boundary cue.** The three zeros mean **prerequisite evidence not
  identified**, never *not yet achieved*.
- **Fallback.** *"A completed version of this grid would still hold questions."*
- **Transition.** *"Twenty-one is abstract. Let's walk the seven domains."*

### Slide 5 — Domain 1: Coordination governance · 1.5 min

- **Opening cue.** *"Purpose, governing document, governed cycle — before any
  tool matters."*
- **Central distinction.** Reference does not constitute approval · a cycle
  trigger is a governance decision, not a software rhythm.
- **Visual-reading cue.** Three cards, deliberately co-equal. **The argument runs
  in order; the slide does not.**
- **Boundary cue.** Harrismith's purpose text, its document split, its cycle
  steps and any frequency **do not transfer**.
- **Fallback.** The universal fallback (§5).
- **Transition.** *"A governed cycle needs people with defined functions."*

### Slide 6 — Domain 2: Roles and authority · 1.5 min

- **Opening cue.** *"Three questions beginning with 'who' — and not one holder."*
- **Central distinction.** Function ≠ person · allocation ≠ holder · allocation ≠
  performance · authority is never inferred from a title or a permission.
- **Visual-reading cue.** Read **function**, then **holder**, on every card.
  *"Notice there is no chart here, and there will not be one."*
- **Boundary cue.** *"An organisation chart is named as evidence we would need.
  It is not evidence we have."*
- **Fallback.** *"I cannot name anyone, and neither can this programme. That is
  the honest position, not an omission."*
- **Transition.** *"Functions act on inputs."*

### Slide 7 — Domain 3: Inputs and federation · 1.5 min

- **Opening cue.** *"Federation is a lens. It moves nothing."*
- **Central distinction.** Visible ≠ selected ≠ suitable ≠ ready · federation ≠
  authorship, approval or publication.
- **Visual-reading cue.** On the `METHOD` card, read **classification**, then the
  qualifier, then **status** — three separate things.
- **Boundary cue.** *"`METHOD` means the distinction is reusable. It does not
  mean Triviron has adopted it."*
- **Fallback.** *"No platform is attributed to Triviron on this slide or
  anywhere in this module."*
- **Transition.** *"Federated inputs get checked."*

### Slide 8 — Domain 4: Checks and tolerances · 1.5 min

- **Opening cue.** *"Interface, check, rule, approver — four governed choices,
  none inherited."*
- **Central distinction.** A software default is not a project tolerance · `TBD`
  is meaningful only inside an established scope.
- **Visual-reading cue.** Spend real time on the **exclusion block**. It is the
  most concrete object on the slide, and it is a list of what may not appear.
- **Boundary cue.** *"Not as starter values, likely values, defaults or
  recommendations — including the `TBD` status itself."*
- **Fallback.** *"I am not going to give you a number, because any number I gave
  you would be Harrismith's or invented."*
- **Transition.** *"Checks produce findings."*

### Slide 9 — Domain 5: Findings, Issues and statuses · 1.5 min

- **Opening cue.** *"Detecting something does not decide what it becomes."*
- **Central distinction.** A clash is one kind of finding · a finding is not
  automatically an Issue · a status label is not technical evidence.
- **Visual-reading cue.** On the `METHOD` card, the distinction is reusable —
  and the status beside it still reads `NOT YET ESTABLISHED`.
- **Boundary cue.** *"No Triviron Issue taxonomy, status model or numbering
  scheme exists in this programme."*
- **Fallback.** The universal fallback (§5).
- **Transition.** *"Once a governed record exists, someone must close it — on
  evidence."*

### Slide 10 — Domain 6: Verification, evidence and completion · 1.5 min

- **Opening cue.** *"A complete cycle is demonstrated, never asserted."*
- **Central distinction.** Ready for verification ≠ verified · one closed matter
  ≠ one complete cycle · completion ≠ zero clashes.
- **Visual-reading cue.** **Name the fence before reading anything inside it.**
  *"This block is Harrismith's evidence about Harrismith."*
- **Boundary cue.** `GCR-006` is **OPEN**, and it is **not a Triviron
  condition**.
- **Fallback.** *"That is Harrismith's record of its own incompleteness. It says
  nothing about Triviron in either direction."*
- **Transition.** *"A completed, verified matter still is not published."*

### Slide 11 — Domain 7: Publication and acceptance boundary · 1.5 min

- **Opening cue.** *"Ability to move a file is not authority to change its
  state."*
- **Central distinction.** Completion ≠ publication ≠ recipient acceptance ·
  verification ≠ authorisation.
- **Visual-reading cue.** Four cells, side by side, **with nothing between
  them**. *"The separation is the content."*
- **Boundary cue.** Read the programme-bounded statement **verbatim**, then the
  two-directional line.
- **Fallback.** *"This programme has not identified a publication authority for
  Triviron. I am not saying there isn't one — I am saying we have not identified
  one."*
- **Transition.** *"Seven domains, twenty-one questions, all typed. What may you
  actually say?"*

### Slide 12 — What the evidence lets you say · 1.5 min

- **Opening cue.** *"Five kinds of statement. Not five rungs."*
- **Central distinction.** Classification (what kind of work the row contains) is
  a separate axis from status (the current governed position).
- **Visual-reading cue.** **Do not read the cards in a line.** Read the hub
  first, then the cards in any order, and say the order is arbitrary.
- **Boundary cue.** The three zeros are **prerequisites not satisfied**, not
  stages awaiting arrival.
- **Fallback.** *"`ESTABLISHED` is not the goal of a ladder. It is a category
  that requires a controlled source we do not have."*
- **Transition.** *"Saying what you know requires a vocabulary for what you
  don't."*

### Slide 13 — Unknowns are controlled work · 1.4 min

- **Opening cue.** *"Unknown is not one status. The reason decides which one."*
- **Central distinction.** Missing evidence ≠ evidence of absence ·
  `NOT YET ESTABLISHED` ≠ `NOT ESTABLISHED` · `TBD` requires an established
  scope.
- **Visual-reading cue.** Four quadrants — **say out loud that there is no first
  and no last**. Then cross the rule and treat `NONE IDENTIFIED` as a different
  kind of thing entirely.
- **Boundary cue.** All 21 statuses read `NOT YET ESTABLISHED` **because that is
  what the evidence supports, and for no other reason**.
- **Fallback.** *"We cannot record `NOT ESTABLISHED` for Triviron today — that
  would require positive evidence of absence, and we have none."*
- **Transition.** *"A backlog of precisely typed unknowns is exactly what a
  decision workshop needs."*

### Slide 14 — From decision backlog to BEP workshop · 1.5 min

- **Opening cue.** **Read the header declaration in full, first, before any
  stage.** *"Teaching workflow — not a current Triviron workflow."*
- **Central distinction.** Evidence ≠ authority · a workshop ≠ automatic
  approval · decision backlog ≠ BEP content.
- **Visual-reading cue.** Walk register 1, **stop at the gate**, then say why the
  gate exists. The final box is reached **only when earned** — no arrow enters
  it.
- **Boundary cue.** *"No actual Triviron workshop or schedule is established in
  this programme, and this diagram does not assert or schedule one."*
- **Fallback.** *"I cannot tell you when, because nothing here schedules
  anything. Module 8 is the next teaching module, not the next event."*
- **Module close.** *"Six modules taught you how a managed project works. This
  one taught you how to ask for one. Module 8 is where we learn to run the
  conversations that earn the answers."*
