### Automatic-fail register reconciliation — `T7-L-R2`

| | Before | After |
|---|---:|---:|
| **Conditions** | **40** | **42** |

**Why the count changed.** The prior register tested type only through condition
28, in the qualitative form *"reduces mandatory governance text below the accepted
type floor"*. That form was **not measurable** and did not reach non-governance
body text at all — which is why a fabrication carrying **146** runs below `14 pt`
could be produced without tripping the register.

| Change | Nature |
|---|---|
| **28** | **Strengthened, not duplicated** — same `M7V-S33` derivation, now an explicit `18 pt` test with the governed run types enumerated |
| **41** | **New** — the deck-wide `14 pt` floor for every visible run. **No prior condition covered it** |
| **42** | **New** — the fit strategies (condensation, reduced spacing, sub-`1.0` leading, permissive producer notes) that cause 28 and 41 |

**No condition was removed, renumbered or weakened.** Conditions 1–27, 29–40 are
**unchanged**, and both accepted STOP registers — teaching `M7-S01`–`M7-S18` and
visual-production `M7V-S01`–`M7V-S34` — are **unchanged**. **No arbitrary failure
has been invented:** 41 and 42 derive from `M7V-S33`, the accepted STOP that
already prohibits setting governance text below `18 pt` and relying on
presentation tricks in place of legible type.
