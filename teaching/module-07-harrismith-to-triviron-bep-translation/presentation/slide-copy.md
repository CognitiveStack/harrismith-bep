## 1. Usage rules

| Rule | Requirement |
|---|---|
| **U1** | **Place this wording. Do not rewrite it.** Where a source declares wording verbatim or mandatory, it is reproduced verbatim below and may not be edited, shortened, resequenced or "improved" |
| **U2** | **Add nothing.** No explanatory prose, no speaker notes, no examples, no compact Triviron illustrations, no captions of your own |
| **U3** | **Convert no question into an answer.** Every `TDR-` line is a matter to decide, phrased as a question, and stays that way |
| **U4** | **Compress only where the accepted source expressly permits it** — chiefly the evidence-kind lists, which may compress to short noun phrases |
| **U5** | **Never compress boundary text.** If space is short, cut content text |
| **U6** | Backticked strings are **exact tokens**. Reproduce the characters, including `≠`, `×` and `—` |
| **U7** | **Classification and status are always two separately labelled lines**, never merged |
| **U8** | Where a line is marked **`TEACHING SYNTHESIS`**, that label appears on the slide in words |

| Slide group | Density |

| 3, 4 | Medium — step labels, grid cells at identifier + 3–6 word topic |

wording live in the accepted register, schema and source files — not on the
slides.**
