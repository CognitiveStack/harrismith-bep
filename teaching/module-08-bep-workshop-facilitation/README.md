## 1. Objective

**Teach the learner to prepare, facilitate, conclude and follow up a BEP
decision workshop so that evidence, discussion, authority, decisions,
unresolved matters and controlled document updates remain distinct and
traceable — without the meeting itself becoming authority or creating
Triviron governance.**

Classification: **`TEACHING SYNTHESIS — NOT GOVERNANCE`.** This objective is
not Harrismith governance. It deliberately says *conclude and follow up*, not
*close*: the repository supports contribution-level closure logic (Working
Process WP8), **not a governed workshop-level closure model** — none may be
implied.

## 2. Central question

**How do you turn a controlled BEP decision backlog into a facilitated
decision-making workshop without allowing the meeting itself to become
authority?**

Classification: **`TEACHING SYNTHESIS — NOT GOVERNANCE`.** A teaching
question only — not a Triviron procedure, not Harrismith controlled
governance, not evidence that any workshop exists, and not a project
requirement.

## 3. Central control principle

**Workshop = structured decision conversation. Workshop ≠ decision
authority.** And along the whole route:
**discussion ≠ governed decision ≠ implementation ≠ verification.**

The principle rests on four source classes that are never collapsed:
controlled BEP support (§8.9, §9.2, §12), controlled decision-register
support, **expressly non-governing** Working Process patterns, and labelled
teaching synthesis — see [`source-inventory.md`](source-inventory.md).

## 4. What Module 8 is

A teaching framework for facilitating BEP decision conversations while
keeping **evidence · discussion · proposals · authority · decisions ·
unresolved matters · actions · implementation · verification** distinct.

## 9. STOP register — Module 8 prohibitions

Governance-approved prohibitions. Each applies to every Module 8 file, slide,
note and derivative.

| # | Prohibition |
|---|---|
| **M8-S01** | **Attendance ≠ authority.** Do not state or imply that attending or participating in a workshop establishes decision authority |
| **M8-S02** | **Facilitator ≠ decision owner.** Facilitating does not automatically make the facilitator decision owner, approver, authoriser or technical owner |
| **M8-S03** | **Chair must not be invented.** No chair function is established by the current source set; none may be invented or implied. Where chairing is discussed as a generic teaching concept, state that chairing a meeting would not by itself establish approval or decision authority — `TEACHING-SAFETY SYNTHESIS — NOT GOVERNANCE` |
| **M8-S04** | **Consensus ≠ authorised decision.** Meeting consensus or agreement in discussion must not be represented as an authorised governance decision |
| **M8-S05** | **Meeting record / minutes ≠ approval.** A meeting record, workshop record or minutes must not automatically be treated as approval, an authorised decision, or governed decision evidence. No governed `minutes` construct exists in the sources; pedagogical use of the word is teaching synthesis |
| **M8-S06** | **Action assignment ≠ technical ownership.** Assigning an action transfers no authorship, technical responsibility or design responsibility |
| **M8-S07** | **Evidence ≠ decision.** Evidence informs; it does not decide, approve, identify authority or create authority |
| **M8-S08** | **Harrismith value ≠ Triviron answer.** No populated Harrismith role, value, workflow, template field, decision, status, tolerance, participant or arrangement may be copied into Triviron as an answer |
| **M8-S09** | **Unknown ≠ `TBD`.** An unknown or unsupported Triviron matter is never converted to `TBD` to populate a workshop record — `TBD` keeps its accepted Module 7 meaning: intentional deferral within an already established governance scope |
| **M8-S10** | **Unresolved ≠ approved.** An `UNRESOLVED` matter does not become approved because it was discussed, consensus seemed to exist, an action was assigned, or a workshop concluded |
| **M8-S11** | **Proposal / working outcome ≠ approved governance.** A candidate is not `ESTABLISHED`, and a Working Process outcome or status — including the word `APPROVED` in a working record — does not by itself create `APPROVED GOVERNANCE`. The controlled governance route remains distinct |
| **M8-S12** | **Teaching workshop ≠ actual Triviron event.** Do not state or imply that a Triviron BEP workshop exists, occurred, is scheduled, has participants or produced decisions unless controlled Triviron evidence establishes that fact. Current programme position: `NONE IDENTIFIED` |
| **M8-S13** | **Module 8 ≠ Triviron BEP.** Module 8 does not create, populate, approve or constitute a Triviron BEP |
| **M8-S14** | **Roadmap ≠ scheduled event.** A roadmap statement that Module 8 teaches workshop facilitation is not evidence that any workshop has been scheduled |
| **M8-S15** | **Tool availability ≠ workshop governance.** Tool/platform availability, permissions, roles or configuration establish no workshop authority or governance |

## 10. Explicit absences — recorded, not invented

The current source set does **not** establish: a chair function; quorum; a
controlled action-due-date convention; a governed workshop-minutes concept; a
per-workshop closure criterion; a fully defined facilitator function (only
the coordination strategy's facilitate-but-not-author behaviour); or a
controlled general BEP-workshop agenda template (only the bounded
publication-planning decision-agenda analogue). **None of these is invented
here.** Their absence does not prevent teaching Module 8 — it marks exactly
where teaching synthesis must remain labelled.
