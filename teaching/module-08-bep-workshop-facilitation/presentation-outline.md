**Objective (accepted architecture — `TEACHING SYNTHESIS — NOT GOVERNANCE`).**
*Teach the learner to prepare, facilitate, conclude and follow up a BEP
decision workshop so that evidence, discussion, authority, decisions,
unresolved matters and controlled document updates remain distinct and
traceable — without the meeting itself becoming authority or creating
Triviron governance.*

**Conceptual spine.** Workshop = structured decision conversation ·
**workshop ≠ decision authority** · **discussion ≠ governed decision ≠
implementation ≠ verification**. Where wording is synthesis, it is labelled.

**Evidence position.** `NO CONTROLLED TRIVIRON EVIDENCE OF AN ACTUAL WORKSHOP
OR LISTED WORKSHOP-GOVERNANCE ITEM IDENTIFIED IN T8-A`; `NONE IDENTIFIED` ≠
`NONE EXISTS`. **No actual workshop is held, scheduled, simulated or implied
by this outline.**

## 10. Slide 8 — During: one decision conversation at a time — DEVELOPED (T8-D)

| # | Field | Value |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Slide number | 8 |
| 2 | Title | During: one decision conversation at a time |
| 3 | Teaching purpose | Move from BEFORE preparation into DURING facilitation; teach the one-matter-at-a-time discipline as **`TEACHING INFERENCE — NOT GOVERNED MEETING PROCEDURE`** — no controlled source establishes it as a meeting rule |
| 4 | Timing allocation | **1.5 min** — allocated, not measured |
| 5 | Audience takeaway | **A useful discussion can occur without decision authority. A governed decision cannot be claimed merely because the discussion occurred** *(the accepted authority checkpoint, carried precisely)* |
| 6 | Visible content | The conversation loop for one prepared matter — expressly a **teaching loop**, not a Harrismith controlled procedure, Triviron procedure or approved workshop workflow: `state the decision question` → `show evidence required / available / missing` → `expose required authority` → `discuss options / reasoning` → `identify what outcome is actually supportable` → `record / route / park appropriately` → `move to next matter`. The authority checkpoint beside it: **where the appropriate authority is not established or engaged, legitimate outputs are** reasoning · clarification · evidence request · action · proposal · recognised decision requirement · unresolved matter — **and discussion alone establishes no governed decision.** Where an appropriate established authority actually exercises its function through the governed route, **that governed act remains separate from attendance, consensus, facilitation and the discussion itself** |
| 7 | Key distinctions | One matter at a time (inference) keeps evidence from bleeding between matters, keeps authority requirements unambiguous, keeps decisions traceable, and keeps unresolved items from disappearing into general discussion *(rationale labelled `TEACHING INFERENCE / SYNTHESIS`)* · discussion ≠ governed decision · consensus alone establishes no authorised decision · record / route / park are three different fates, each legitimate |
| 8 | Source basis | Framework §3 (accepted architecture — the loop's per-step basis classes) and §4 (the authority checkpoint, verbatim); working-pattern verbatims behind steps 4–6 (*"Options are not decisions"*, *"A proposal is not an approval"* — `FOR REVIEW · Authority: None · Not approved`); BEP §8.9 (controlled) behind the record step; the one-at-a-time rationale is labelled inference/synthesis |
| 9 | Framework relationship | DURING steps 1–9 and the §4 authority checkpoint — this slide is the framework's DURING phase at teaching altitude |
| 10 | Applicable STOP conditions | **`M8-S01`**, **`M8-S04`** (consensus is never automatic authority), **`M8-S05`**, **`M8-S07`**, **`M8-S10`** (parked/unresolved stays unresolved), **`M8-S11`**, **`M8-S12`** |
| 11 | What must not be said | That the loop is a governed meeting procedure anywhere; that no decision can ever occur during a workshop *(the boundary is that discussion alone establishes none)*; that consensus, attendance or facilitation constitute the governed act; that parking a matter resolves it |
| 12 | Transition to next slide | *"Inside every one of those conversations, four words keep getting confused — discussion, option, proposal, decision. Next slide separates them for good."* |
