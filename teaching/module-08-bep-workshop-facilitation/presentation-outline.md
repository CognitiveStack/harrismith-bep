**Objective (accepted architecture — `TEACHING SYNTHESIS — NOT GOVERNANCE`).**
*Teach the learner to prepare, facilitate, conclude and follow up a BEP
decision workshop so that evidence, discussion, authority, decisions,
unresolved matters and controlled document updates remain distinct and
traceable — without the meeting itself becoming authority or creating
Triviron governance.*

**Conceptual spine.** Workshop = structured decision conversation ·
**workshop ≠ decision authority** · **discussion ≠ governed decision ≠
implementation ≠ verification**. Where wording is synthesis, it is labelled.

**Evidence position.** `NO CONTROLLED TRIVIRON EVIDENCE OF AN ACTUAL WORKSHOP
OR LISTED WORKSHOP-GOVERNANCE ITEM IDENTIFIED IN T8-A`; `NONE IDENTIFIED` ≠
`NONE EXISTS`. **No actual workshop is held, scheduled, simulated or implied
by this outline.**
