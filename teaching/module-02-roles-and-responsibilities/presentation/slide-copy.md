Everything below is intended to be **visible on the slide**. What the presenter

## How to read this file

| Element | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Title** | The slide title |
| **Subtitle** | Second line, where used |
| **Main statement** | The one sentence the slide argues |
| **Supporting items** | Labels or single-line bullets — **maximum five** |
| **Diagram labels** | Text inside the visual |
| **Call-out** | A boxed or emphasised line |
| **Footer** | Small, low-contrast evidence or source note |
| **Visible warning** | A required on-slide label — used on **7 of 14 slides** |

**Density limits:** one main message · maximum five supporting items · no
paragraph blocks · no raw matrices · no dense responsibility register · no full
sixteen-authority table on the main Slide 12 · no raw source extracts · no
generic stock role definitions. Roughly **45 visible words per slide** excluding
diagram labels.

**Retain necessary distinctions even where they add a little copy.** Slides 6, 12
and 14 exceed the guide deliberately.

## 2. Slide 2

**Title**
> A role is not the same as a job title

**Main statement**
> These are functions, not job titles and not people

**Supporting items** — *three concepts, not interchangeable*
- **Project function** — what must be done or decided
- **Job title** — a label an organisation gives a person
- **Person** — who performs the function

**Diagram labels** (`M02-S02`)
> *One participant — no holder established*
> **Function A** — BIM Manager · approves a governance change
> **Function B** — CDE Administrator · applies it in the platform
> *different act · different authority*

**Call-out**
> Combining functions does not merge them.

**Footer**
> BEP §4.6, §5.11 · delegation must be explicit; platform access is not
> delegation

**Visible warning** — none.
