## 3. Design approach

### 3.1 Format and construction

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Aspect | **16:9** |
| Reference canvas | **960 × 540 pt** |
| Origin | **Top left**; `x` increases right, `y` increases down |
| Title zone | **`y = 0–72` — reserved.** No content beneath the title baseline |
| Side margins | **≥ 48 pt** |
| Minimum visible type | **14 pt** |
| Principal labels | **≥ 18 pt** |
| Text contrast | **≥ 4.5 : 1** |
| Border and connector contrast | **≥ 3 : 1** |
| Meaning by colour | **Never.** Every status is carried by a **word** |
| Register | **Professional BIM-management.** No marketing styling |
| Construction | **Native PowerPoint only** — shapes, text boxes, tables, borders, lines, connectors |

### 3.2 Prohibited throughout the deck

**No SmartArt · no Mermaid · no SVG · no PNG · no JPG · no PDF import · no
screenshot · no external icon · no external imagery · no platform image · no
stock photography · no logo · no live-evidence capture.**

**No person imagery of any kind**, anywhere in the deck — and specifically not on
Slide 8.

### 3.3 Status-label rules

**Governance status and implementation status are two separate things and must
remain separately visible.** A slide that carries both carries both.

**A required status label appears on the slide where its source requires it.**
**A load-bearing status may never be moved into the presenter notes.**

**None of the following may be abbreviated, paraphrased or replaced by a symbol:**

- `CONTROLLED GOVERNANCE`
- `PROPOSED GOVERNANCE`
- `ESTABLISHED ALLOCATION`
- `UNRESOLVED`
- `BLOCKED`
- `IMPLEMENTATION UNVERIFIED`
- `NOT DEMONSTRATED`
- `NOT YET ESTABLISHED`
- `NO ALLOCATION MADE`
- `INDEPENDENCE — NOT ESTABLISHED`

Two further status words are used where the sources require them and are subject
to the same rule: **`CONDITIONAL`** and **`OBSERVED — QUALIFIED`**.

### 3.4 Connector-semantics register

**Connectors carry meaning in this deck. Forms that mean different things are
never normalised into one.** A **missing** connector is **intentional** wherever
the source specifies no relationship.

| Form | Meaning | Slides used |
|---|---|---|
| **No connector** | **The source states no relationship.** Deliberate, and load-bearing | **1, 2, 6, 8, 9, 10, 12, 13, 14** |
| **Directional dependency** — single-headed arrow labelled *depends on* | One resource consumes from another. **One direction only; no arrow returns** | **3** |
| **Resolution chain** — single-headed arrow meaning *resolves to* | `party → task team → discipline → container` | **5** |
| **Aggregation** — **dashed** line, marked temporary and non-authoring | `COORD-01` aggregates from the six containers. **Implies no ownership and no authorship** | **5** |
| **Overlap without merged function** — spatial overlap, **no connector** | One participant may span two roles while the functional boundary stays visible | **8** |
| **`uses`** — vertical connector, **labelled `uses`** | An event **uses or depends upon** a transition. **Never identity** | **11** |
| **Blocked relationship** — connector drawn **with a visible break**, labelled `BLOCKED` | The dependency exists and **cannot be traversed** | **11** |
| **Enclosure** — drawn boundary, no fill, visible border | Membership. Three task teams inside **one** party | **5** |
| **Leader line** — short line from a call-out to a row | **Points at a row and means nothing else** | **4, 7** |
| **Refused / prohibited relationship** | Explicitly forbidden, and its absence is the teaching point | **3** (no R/A/C/I mapping) · **10** (no sequence) · **14** (no Harrismith → Triviron line) |

**No equals sign. No double-headed arrow. No shared node between an event and a
transition.**

### 3.5 Accessibility, deck-wide

Every slide carries: a **logical reading order** · a **screen-reader
description** · **status meaning in words** · **no information by colour alone**
· projection readability at the stated minimums · **connector meaning carried by
form and label**, never by colour · and warnings legible at projection scale.

**Where a visual separation is load-bearing — a gutter, an enclosure, an overlap,
a rule — it must also be described in words**, because visual separation is
invisible to a screen reader.
