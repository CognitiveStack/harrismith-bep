### 2.1 How the sequence was derived

The increment proposed a provisional architecture. **Four changes were made on
source evidence**, and the count, the progression and the twenty-minute total
are unchanged.

| # | Change | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Slide 3 introduces all three resources before any is examined** | All three resources define themselves *by contrast with the other two*. Teaching `S2` first, in isolation, would require re-teaching the contrast three times |
| 2 | **A dedicated slide (8) for role combination** | `S2` §5 is the single clearest statement in the source set that allocation is not performance, and it has no natural home inside the grammar slides |
| 3 | **A dedicated slide (13) for how absence is recorded** | `H-D11` found that **no Harrismith matrix or schedule contains a blank**. That inverts the expected teaching point — the lesson is not *how to read a blank* but *how a discipline of naming absence works* — and it needs its own slide |
| 4 | **Slide 11 separates event from transition before Slide 12 explains the block** | `TRN-E03` cannot be explained safely until the audience can distinguish it from `T4`. Module 4 established the distinction; Slide 11 re-establishes it in delivery-planning terms |

### 2.2 Three notes on the allocation

- **Section D is the longest at 6.0 minutes.** Delivery planning is where the
  module's unresolved matters live, and where the temptation to complete a row
  is strongest.
- **Slide 12 must not be cut.** It is the module's honesty slide, and its
  message — **five independent blocking matters, not one** — is the point most
  easily lost. **If time is short, recover from Slide 8 or Slide 13.**
- **Slide 6 must stay at concept level.** No suitability, status or naming code
  set exists; there is no coding standard to teach.

**On-slide copy**

> ## Three resources. Three questions. One refusal.
>
> | Resource | The question it answers — *in its own words* |

> | **IM Responsibility Matrix** | *"who performs which information-management function?"* |
> | **Model / Information Responsibility Matrix** | *"who produces and maintains which information container?"* |
> | **Information Delivery Schedule** | *what is exchanged, at what event, to whom, why, in what form* |
>
> **Each states what it does *not* answer.** The disclaimers are mutual.
>
> ### The refusal — BEP §5.12
>
> > **"RACI is not adopted."**
> > Because it *"collapses"* **checking from authorising**, and **coordinating
> > from performing** — *"distinctions this BEP depends on."*

>
> **Schedule → Container matrix → Function matrix**

> Checker to the function matrix. **Nothing depends on the schedule.**

> *"No container is scheduled that does not exist in that matrix."*
>
> ### The word *row* means three different things
>
> a **function** · a **container** · a **container within an event**
>
> **Unresolved:** no source defines how a change in one propagates to the others.
