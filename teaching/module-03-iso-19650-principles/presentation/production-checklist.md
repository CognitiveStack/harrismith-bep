# Module 3 — Production Checklist

**Status:** Build checklist for the producer. **Not governance.**

Work through this **while building**, and again before handover. The review
checklist in [`review-checklist.md`](review-checklist.md) is a different
activity, performed on the finished deck.

---

## 1. Global checks

- [ ] **Fourteen slides**, no more and no fewer
- [ ] **16:9**
- [ ] **Exact titles** as specified in [`deck-specification.md`](deck-specification.md) §4
- [ ] **Timing totals exactly 20.0 planned minutes** — 4.0 · 4.5 · 4.5 · 3.0 · 4.0
- [ ] Consistent title position across all fourteen
- [ ] **One principal message per slide**
- [ ] Projection-readable text throughout
- [ ] **Original visuals only** — every diagram built as native shapes

### 1.1 Copyright

- [ ] **No ISO diagram, table or figure reproduced, redrawn, adapted or
      reconstructed** — including from memory, from search results, from a
      third-party version, or as a "simplified" derivative with changed styling
- [ ] **No ISO heading, section name or structural term** beyond registered
      document titles and part numbers
- [ ] **No ISO branding used as decoration**
- [ ] **No clause number anywhere in the deck**

### 1.2 Source integrity

- [ ] **No public scope presented as a requirement**
- [ ] **No ISO definition invented** — including *information container* and
      *level of information need*
- [ ] **No requirement acronym** — no client-, exchange-, asset- or
      project-level requirement abbreviation
- [ ] **Every UK guidance item carries `Official implementation guidance — UK
      context`** — Slides 5, 6, 7, 8
- [ ] Source-class labels present wherever a panel could be mistaken for ISO's
      own
- [ ] Drafts appear **only** as a subordinate footer on Slide 3

### 1.3 Claims

- [ ] **No Harrismith conformity conclusion — and none of non-conformity**
- [ ] **No `partially compliant`, `mostly aligned`, `ISO-ready`, percentage or
      maturity level** anywhere
- [ ] **No compliance score, tick, cross, traffic light or progress bar**
- [ ] **No Triviron fact** — jurisdiction, client, procurement route, software,
      dates, appointments, stage or existing alignment
- [ ] **No platform screenshot, product logo used as evidence, or fabricated
      image**
- [ ] **No claim that any rendered asset, external image or live observation
      exists**

### 1.4 Connector semantics

- [ ] **Solid arrows only where supported progression exists**
- [ ] **Dashed arrows for blocked, unresolved or future relationships**
- [ ] **Plain lines for relationships without direction or authority**
- [ ] **No connector at all between the two vocabularies on Slide 8**

## 3. Deliberate incompleteness — STOP conditions

**Any producer action that completes, aligns, balances or fills one of these
contrary to the source is a STOP failure. Do not hand over. Revert the change.**

| # | STOP if | Slide |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Vocabulary rows have been **aligned**, or any connector drawn between the columns | **8** |
| 2 | `Shared → Published` has been drawn as a **complete or continuous route** | **10** |
| 3 | The **evidence column has been populated**, or a date, tick or recipient added | **11** |
| 4 | The **evidence stage has been completed**, or assessment moved **into** the chain | **12** |
| 5 | **Band 1 holds more than one item**, or the bands have been resized to balance | **13** |

**A STOP failure is not a style disagreement.** Each of these asserts something
the evidence does not support, and four of the five can be committed **by layout
alone, with no word spoken**.

**If in doubt, leave it incomplete and raise it.** The source file for each slide
records why.

## 4. Accessibility

- [ ] **Sufficient contrast** for projection in a lit room
- [ ] **Meaning does not depend on colour** — every distinction is also carried
      by shape, label, line style or position
- [ ] **Solid and dashed lines are distinguishable at projection scale** — this
      is load-bearing on Slides 10, 11 and 12, where dashed means *blocked*
- [ ] **Small source and jurisdiction labels remain readable** — they are
      content, not decoration, and must not be shrunk to fit
- [ ] **Projection-scale tested** — viewed at full screen, from the back of a
      room, before handover
- [ ] **Text alternatives in the notes pane** where a visual carries meaning that
      the copy does not — particularly Slides 8, 10 and 13

**Colour independence matters more than usual here.** The deck deliberately
avoids green and red, so a viewer who cannot distinguish line styles has no other
cue for *blocked*.
