## 5. The five production prohibitions — carried exactly

**Any producer action that completes, aligns, balances or fills one of these is a
content failure. Revert it.**

### Slide 8 — `M03-S08`

> **Rows do not align across the gutter.** Deliberately offset. **`lead appointed
> party` is never drawn directly opposite `Lead Delivery Party`.** `appointed
> party` appears with **nothing** opposite it — **the empty space is the
> teaching**. **No arrows between unverified terms. No colour-coded pairing, tick
> or equivalence symbol.**

**Build as placed shapes, not as a table object.** A table object aligns rows by
construction, and alignment **is** the assertion this visual exists to refuse.

### Slide 10 — `M03-S10`

> **`WIP → Shared` is solid** — and only because the authorising function is
> established. **`Shared → Published` is dashed or visibly halted. Never solid,
> never dotted-but-continuous, never a faded arrow that reads as *in progress*.**
> `Published → Record / Retained` is unreachable.

**Every design instinct will push toward completing that arrow, because a broken
chain looks like an unfinished drawing. It is not unfinished. It is accurate.**

### Slide 11 — `M03-S11`

> **The evidence-status column exists and is empty for every event.** Its
> emptiness is the slide's argument; a visual without it is a plan pretending to
> be a record. **No actual dates** — timing is *event-triggered* or *TBD*.
> **Recipient and acceptance authority stay unpopulated.**

### Slide 12 — `M03-S12`

> **The evidence stage is drawn open** — not crossed out, not red, not marked
> failed. **Conformity assessment sits outside the chain**, attached by a dashed
> annotation, **never as an eighth step**. If positions are marked at all, mark
> **only the last two**.

### Slide 13 — `M03-S13`

> **All four bands are the same size**, including band 4. **Band 1 holds exactly
> one item**, and **its near-emptiness is the finding**. The empty space is
> deliberate and is **not to be filled, padded or balanced**. **No band is
> coloured. The bands are not ordered as a journey.**

**A near-empty band beside a nine-item band looks unbalanced, and every design
instinct will try to correct it.** The imbalance is the content.
