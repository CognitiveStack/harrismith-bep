# Module 3 — Review Checklist

**Status:** Review checklist for the first built PowerPoint. **Not governance.**

Performed **on the finished deck**, by someone who did not build it. The
production checklist in
[`production-checklist.md`](production-checklist.md) is a different activity,
performed during the build.

**Module 3 review has not been performed.** Nothing below has been run.

---

## 1. Source review

**Does the deck represent its evidence honestly?**

- [ ] **Public scope remains scope.** No scope statement has become a
      requirement, an obligation or a clause
- [ ] **Drafts remain subordinate.** Revision status appears only as a footer on
      Slide 3 — no third column, no draft content, no predicted date
- [ ] **Guidance is UK-labelled.** Every `X5`/`X6` item on Slides 5, 6, 7 and 8
      carries `Official implementation guidance — UK context`
- [ ] **Project evidence is not presented as ISO.** Harrismith wording on Slides
      2, 9, 10, 11 and 12 is attributed to this project's BEP
- [ ] **Synthesis is identifiable.** Teaching wording — Slide 1's call-out, Slide
      12's chain, Slide 14 throughout — is not delivered as sourced
- [ ] **No ISO figure has been reconstructed**, including as a restyled or
      "simplified" derivative
- [ ] No clause number appears anywhere
- [ ] No ISO definition has been supplied for *information container* or *level
      of information need*

## 2. Content review

**Does the deck teach what the module argues?**

- [ ] **Information-management framing** — Slide 1 removes the software
      assumption before anything else
- [ ] **Life-cycle distinction** — Slide 4 shows the asset's span, and refuses
      the single-eternal-model reading
- [ ] **Requirements-first logic** — Slide 5's sequence runs one way, and
      production is never first
- [ ] **Purpose-dependent quantity** — Slide 6 treats **both** ends as failures
- [ ] **Function versus title** — Slide 7 places the job title outside the chain
- [ ] **The vocabulary gap** — Slide 8 asserts no mapping, and shows the absence
- [ ] **Container responsibility** — Slide 9 shows mixed containers, each owned,
      with federation not merging ownership
- [ ] **Blocked publication** — Slide 10 shows one working route and one stopped
      one, with the block explained as deliberate
- [ ] **Planned versus actual** — Slide 11 shows a plan, and no delivery
- [ ] **Translation versus conformity** — Slide 12 shows the path, with
      assessment outside it
- [ ] **Mapping categories** — Slide 13 shows four equal bands and no conclusion
      either way
- [ ] **Question-only Triviron close** — Slide 14 asks and does not answer

## 3. Visual review

**Does the layout assert anything the words do not?**

- [ ] **Source labels readable at projection scale** — not shrunk to fit
- [ ] **No hierarchy introduced** where the source has none — particularly Slide
      7, which must not become an org chart
- [ ] **No equivalence introduced** — particularly Slide 8, where alignment
      alone would commit the claim
- [ ] **Connector semantics correct** — solid only for supported progression,
      dashed for blocked or unresolved, plain for relationships without
      authority, **nothing between the Slide 8 columns**
- [ ] **No compliance colours** — no green, amber or red carrying meaning
- [ ] **No scorecard, percentage, maturity ladder or progress indicator**
- [ ] **Empty and blocked states remain empty or blocked** — Slides 10, 11 and 12
- [ ] **Slide 13's imbalance retained** — band 1 near-empty, four bands identical
- [ ] **Slide 14 unanswered** — no populated field, no greyed example

## 4. Teaching review

**Would an audience leave with the right understanding?**

- [ ] The audience would understand **ISO 19650 is not software**
- [ ] **Part 1 and Part 2 are distinguished safely** — by stated scope, not by
      invented contents
- [ ] **UK guidance is not confused with ISO** — a viewer could say which is
      which
- [ ] **Harrismith is useful without being labelled compliant** — the analogues
      teach, and none is presented as evidence about the standard
- [ ] **`partially compliant` language is rejected**, on the slide and in the
      notes
- [ ] **The licensed copy is presented as necessary but not sufficient**
- [ ] **Alignment is shown to require evidence and an authorised basis** — and a
      competent assessor
- [ ] A viewer photographing any single slide could not reasonably present it as
      an ISO document or a compliance claim

## 5. Rehearsal readiness

- [ ] **Cues are speakable** — fragments, not paragraphs to read aloud
- [ ] **Transitions are clear** between all fourteen slides and all five sections
- [ ] **Likely questions have safe answers** in the notes pane
- [ ] **A timing allocation exists** for every slide, totalling 20.0
- [ ] Both closings present — **30-second and 10-second**
- [ ] Recovery sentences present for all five sections
- [ ] **No rehearsal result is claimed**, and no timing is recorded as measured

## 6. Automatic fail conditions

**Any one of these fails the review. The deck returns to production.**

| # | Fail condition | Where it would show |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **A reconstructed ISO figure** — including a restyled or third-party derivative | Anywhere; most likely Slides 3, 4, 5 |
| 2 | **Public scope presented as requirements** | Slide 3 |
| 3 | **A missing UK jurisdiction label** | Slides 5, 6, 7, 8 |
| 4 | **Aligned vocabulary rows**, or any connector between the columns | **Slide 8** |
| 5 | **A complete `Shared → Published` route** | **Slide 10** |
| 6 | **Populated evidence fields**, or an added date, tick or recipient | **Slide 11** |
| 7 | **A completed conformity chain**, or assessment moved into it | **Slide 12** |
| 8 | **An additional item in Slide 13 band 1**, or rebalanced bands | **Slide 13** |
| 9 | **A percentage, maturity score, tick or traffic light** | Anywhere |
| 10 | **A populated Triviron answer** | **Slide 14** |
| 11 | **An invented ISO, Harrismith or Triviron fact** | Anywhere |

**Conditions 4 to 8 are the layout failures.** Each can be committed **without a
word being spoken**, by a template, a designer or an autofit. **Check them
visually, not by reading the copy.**
